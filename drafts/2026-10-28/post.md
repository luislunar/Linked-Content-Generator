A lapsed SAM.gov registration can freeze your GSA payments — even if your Schedule contract is in perfect standing.

This is the most common maintenance trap I see for small businesses already on the Schedule. Everyone focuses on the hard part — getting approved, catalog built, pricing negotiated. Then they go quiet.

Here's what actually happens when SAM.gov expires mid-contract: agencies can't issue new task orders against your vehicle, existing payment processing stalls, and your contract gets flagged for review. Not hypothetically. This happens.

SAM.gov registrations expire every 12 months. The system sends one email, 60 days out. If that email lands in the wrong folder, your only warning is a payment that doesn't arrive.

The fix takes ten minutes: pull your SAM expiration date right now and set a calendar reminder 90 days before. Log in, renew proactively. Don't wait for the system to tell you.

Your GSA contract doesn't run on autopilot. Neither does the registration underneath it.
