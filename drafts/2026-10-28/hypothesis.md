This draft tests the `alert` hook pattern on a `compliance` topic (SAM.gov lapse → payment freeze) using prose format with no bullets — matching the hot_take type spec. The hook opens with a specific, counterintuitive claim: your GSA Schedule can be active and still not pay you. This creates immediate tension for any small business owner who has a Schedule but hasn't thought about SAM maintenance.

Reactions are expected to move because the claim is surprising and slightly anxiety-inducing for holders who haven't checked their SAM expiration recently. Comments should rise from the closing question, which asks readers to name the compliance deadline that catches them off guard — specific enough to pull real answers (SAM vs. Reps & Certs is a genuine debate), open enough that any GovCon participant has a response.
