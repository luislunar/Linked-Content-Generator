"Once you're on the GSA Schedule, the hard work is done."

I hear this every few months. It's one of the most expensive myths in GovCon.

Here's what's actually true: an active Schedule demands ongoing work — annual SAM.gov renewals, pricing modifications when your commercial rates shift, Trade Agreements Act reviews on new offerings, and audit responses when GSA pulls your file. Miss any one of those, and GSA can cancel your contract without warning.

The Schedule is a tool, not a trophy. It needs maintenance to keep its value.

What's the last compliance task you completed on your GSA contract? Be honest. 😅
