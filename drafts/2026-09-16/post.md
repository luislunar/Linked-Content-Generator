Before you lock in your GSA Schedule pricing — read this.

I've seen it more times than I can count. A small business submits a solid application: clean financials, strong past performance, well-organized offer. And then the pricing package either stalls the negotiation or gets approved at rates that quietly erode margins for years.

Here are 5 pricing traps worth knowing before you submit:

1. **Anchoring to your commercial rate without thinking it through.** Your GSA price is tied to your Basis of Award — the pricing relationship you establish with your most favored commercial customer. Discount privately later, and GSA may be entitled to that same rate.

2. **Underestimating the Price Reduction Clause.** This clause follows you throughout the contract period. Every time you offer a better deal anywhere else, you could trigger a matching obligation to the government.

3. **Vague unit pricing.** "Discounted from catalog" is not a price. Contracting officers need clean, auditable unit rates. Ambiguity stalls approval — or invites a lower counter-offer.

4. **Skipping the competitive benchmark.** Your GSA rates are public. Competitors can see them. Price too high and you won't win work. Price too low and you put downward pressure on your commercial business.

5. **No escalation clause.** Without an Economic Price Adjustment provision, you're absorbing year-3 and year-4 costs at year-1 rates. Build the ramp in now.

The pricing section is the one most people rush. It's also the one that's hardest to renegotiate after the fact.
