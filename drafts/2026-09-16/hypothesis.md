This draft tests a `question` hook pattern on the `pricing` topic using a numbered `tips_list` format. The `question` hook ("Before you lock in your GSA Schedule pricing — read this.") was chosen because the last three posts all opened with declarative statements, making a question mandatory per cadence rules — and pricing is exactly the topic where readers feel uncertain enough to pause before acting. The numbered format (1–5, no arrows) deliberately breaks the visual rhythm of the recent prose-heavy posts. Pricing is a high-anxiety topic for the ICP: they worry about locking in wrong rates they can't easily fix later. The five traps are concrete and ranked by how often I see them in real submissions, which should generate comments from readers who recognize their own past mistakes — particularly around the Price Reduction Clause and the missing escalation provision. Expected lift: moderate-to-strong `reactions` (the traps are recognizable and somewhat uncomfortable) and above-average `comments` (pricing war stories are easy to share and don't require disclosing sensitive numbers).
