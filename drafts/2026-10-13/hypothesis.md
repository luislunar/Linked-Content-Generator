This draft tests a `blunt` hook on a `client_story` format covering the `pricing` topic — a combination not recently run. The opener ("Badly priced GSA Schedules are worse than no Schedule at all") creates immediate tension for owners who are mid-application or evaluating whether their commercial rates translate to federal. The story arc moves deliberately: relatable DIY attempt → specific pricing mistake (commercial-benchmark fallacy) → concrete GSA Focus intervention → generalizable lesson. Expected to drive reactions from owners who recognize the pricing mistake in themselves, and comments from people sharing their own BOA confusion or asking about the Basis of Award process. The `single-05-beforeafter` template reinforces the before/after transformation narrative visually and uses a cream background, providing contrast after two consecutive dark-bg cards (single-06 on 10-13, single-04 on 10-12).
