Nobody talks enough about how much federal money is still moving to small businesses — and how early the real work starts.

In Q2 2026, the Department of Defense alone awarded $21.9 billion to small businesses. One quarter. One agency. Add VA ($3.6B), DHS ($3.2B), USDA ($2.5B), HHS ($2.2B) — the market did not slow down.

The companies capturing that money weren't waiting on RFQs to appear in SAM.gov. They were in the pipeline months earlier — responding to sources sought notices, attending industry days, getting on agency radars before the solicitation was ever written. By the time the RFQ dropped, they were already the known option.

Most small businesses engage at the last step. The ones winning engage at the first.

The federal pipeline doesn't favor the fastest bidder. It favors whoever showed up before the competition knew there was a race.

If your pipeline starts when a solicitation is posted, you're already behind.
