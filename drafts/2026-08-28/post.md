I got a call last year from a CEO with eight years of solid commercial revenue, a sharp team, and zero federal past performance.

She was convinced the government market was off-limits until she'd built some kind of track record. I hear this constantly.

Here's what actually happened: She spent a month documenting her capabilities clearly — not a proposal, just a tight, honest picture of what her team could do. Then she identified two primes chasing task orders in her space and reached out to both. One responded. By month six she'd signed her first subcontract on a DoD task order.

No federal past performance. No stars-aligned timing. Just a teaming agreement with a prime who needed exactly what she had.

This is how most small businesses actually enter the federal market — not by winning a prime contract out of nowhere, but by proving value to someone who already has the relationship. The catch is you have to be visible first. A prime can't choose you if they don't know you exist.

The federal market doesn't require a five-year runway. It requires documentation, the right contacts, and the patience to follow through.

What's the one thing keeping you from making that first outreach to a prime in your space?
