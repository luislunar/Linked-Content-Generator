This draft tests the `blunt` hook pattern on a `behind_scenes` post type — a format not used in the recent rotation. The process reveal is intentionally specific: a named tool (USASpending.gov), a named artifact ("recompete hot list"), and concrete numbers (40–50 rows, 120-day window) designed to feel like a genuine insider look rather than generic advice. Short prose format (under 130 words) tests whether brevity + specificity outperforms our longer educational posts. Expected to move: **comments** (high — the community question "what's your current system?" invites peers to share their own process, which is more engaging than opinion questions) and **reactions** (medium — the "I didn't know this existed" realization moment tends to drive saves and likes). If this outperforms on comments vs. the 08-24 hot_take, it validates that process-reveal posts generate more dialogue than analysis posts.
