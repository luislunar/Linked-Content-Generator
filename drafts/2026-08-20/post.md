I had a call two weeks ago with a business owner who told me her WOSB certification wasn't working. She'd had it for 14 months.

The first thing I asked: how many capability statements had she sent to contracting officers in that time? How many sources-sought notices had she responded to? How many CO relationships had she actively built?

You can guess the answer.

Here's the thing about set-aside certifications — 8(a), WOSB, SDVOSB, HUBZone — they don't call the government for you. They tell the government you're eligible *when the agency is looking*. But agencies still need a reason to find YOU in that pool. The pipeline is yours to build.

The certification is a starting gun. The companies I've watched win in their first 1–2 years? They treat it that way. They're registered in agency-specific vendor databases. They're showing up at industry days. They're responding to RFIs months before RFPs drop.

The certificate is proof of eligibility. Everything that follows is your job.

If you have a set-aside certification — 8(a), WOSB, SDVOSB, HUBZone — what's one concrete action you take each week to stay visible to contracting officers at your target agencies?
