Before you apply for 8(a), WOSB, or HUBZone — ask yourself one question first.

Am I actually positioned to compete?

A certification multiplies your access to set-aside contracts. It doesn't create demand for your services, write your capability statement, or teach you how to respond to an RFQ.

I've seen companies spend 18 months chasing 8(a) status while ignoring the fundamentals that actually win federal contracts.

Get the cert. But get positioned first.
