This draft tests the `question` hook pattern on the `certifications` topic using the `inspiration` format. The core tension — "certified ≠ positioned" — targets a common mistake: small business owners treating a certification as a demand-generator rather than an access-multiplier. The question hook ("Before you apply... ask yourself one question") is designed to create a small moment of self-doubt in readers who are mid-process or considering a cert application, which should drive saves and comments. The community question ("What's one thing you wish you'd built before...") invites experience-share responses from people who have been through the cert process, which should lift comments. Expected impact: strong comment volume from govcon practitioners sharing their "I wish I'd known" moments; moderate reactions from ICP members who recognize themselves in the framing.
