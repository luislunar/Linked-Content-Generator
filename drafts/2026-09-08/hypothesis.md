This draft tests a **blunt hook + tips_list format on the pricing topic**. The opening statement leads with a pain the ICP recognizes immediately — getting approved but then struggling to win — which creates a gap the reader wants closed. The numbered format (rather than bullets) signals a complete, scannable framework, which tends to drive saves and shares more than prose. The five items cover both mechanical mistakes (wrong rate, no documentation) and the longer-tail ones (Price Reduction Clause, broad labor categories) that more experienced readers will recognize and validate with comments. Expected impact: the specific, slightly technical nature of items 2–4 should drive reactions from people who've been through this and want to signal expertise, plus comments from people who hit one of these and want to share their story. The community question is deliberately experience-anchored ("biggest pricing surprise") to pull in stories rather than generic responses.
