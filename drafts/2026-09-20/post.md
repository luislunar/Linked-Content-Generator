A client messaged me three weeks ago in a full panic.

She runs a 12-person IT services firm. Two-year GSA Schedule holder. Active delivery orders, invoices going out every month. Then payments stopped, and her CO went quiet. She spent three weeks assuming it was an agency budget hold.

It wasn't.

Her SAM.gov registration had expired 11 days earlier. No warning she caught. No hard stop on her GSA Advantage listing. Just a clock hitting zero — and every active payment freezing automatically until the registration renewed.

The renewal itself took 48 hours once we found it. The three weeks of confusion before that cost her $42,000 in delayed payments.

Here's what nobody tells you at Schedule approval: SAM.gov doesn't remind you. It expires every 12 months, requires a manual renewal, and when it lapses, the government doesn't send a courtesy email — it freezes every payment tied to your UEI until you fix it.

If you're a Schedule holder, do these two things right now:
→ Log in to SAM.gov and look up your expiration date.
→ Set a calendar reminder 60 days before it hits.
