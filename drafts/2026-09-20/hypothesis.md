This draft tests the story hook pattern applied to the SAM topic — instead of leading with a warning or a checklist, it opens with a client in panic mode, which creates immediate identification for any Schedule holder who has ever had a payment delayed without explanation. Expected to move both reactions (the before/after tension earns saves and likes) and comments (the honest-check CTA invites specific, low-friction admissions rather than abstract opinions).
