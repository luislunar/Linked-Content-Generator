This draft tests an alert-hook tips_list on the pricing topic — a combination not used in the last 5 days. The hook opens with hidden consequences rather than a direct list announcement, designed to create tension before the reader reaches the numbered items.

The 5 mistakes were chosen to span the full lifecycle of a GSA pricing error: submission (wrong baseline, Price Reductions Clause), post-award behavior (pricing to win vs. sustain, price realism), and ongoing maintenance (stale price list). This arc gives practitioners at every stage something to recognize and act on.

The community question asks for a personal unlearning moment rather than a generic reaction, which should drive story-based comments from holders and applicants alike. Expected outcome: moderate reaction volume from practitioners who recognize the mistakes, and a comment thread anchored by specific pricing experiences — comments being the higher-value engagement metric for this profile.
