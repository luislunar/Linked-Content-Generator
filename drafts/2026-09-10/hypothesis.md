This draft tests the `story` hook pattern combined with a `personal_story` format on the `gsa_basics` topic — a departure from the last three posts, which all opened with declarative statements (contrarian, blunt, myth-bust). The anecdote structure — a specific CEO, a specific question, a specific misunderstanding corrected — is designed to feel like a genuine conversation rather than an educational breakdown. The community question ("What surprised you once you dug into it?") invites direct experience-sharing, which should lift comment volume versus the more abstract CTAs in recent posts. Reactions should hold steady; comments are the primary lever this draft is pulling.
