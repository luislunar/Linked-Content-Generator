There is no "best" contract vehicle. I've been saying this for 18 years.

Every week I get some version of: "Should I do GSA MAS or OASIS+? What about SEWP?" The real question isn't which vehicle is best — it's which one your target agencies actually use to buy what you sell.

Department of Defense buys IT through different channels than VA. SEWP dominates NASA and Treasury tech buys. OASIS+ is the professional services workhorse for many civilian agencies. GSA MAS gives you the broadest coverage if you're still mapping your buyer landscape.

Here's what I tell every client before they chase a new vehicle: pick 3–5 agencies you genuinely want to work with, pull the last 10 contract awards in your NAICS code, and trace which vehicles they used. 90% of the time, the right answer becomes obvious.
