This draft tests a `blunt` hook pattern on the `vehicles` topic using a `hot_take` format. The opener ("There is no 'best' contract vehicle") is designed to arrest readers who are actively in the vehicle-selection phase — a high-intent, specific decision point where people are prone to endless comparison loops. The "follow the buyer" reframe is immediately actionable: a concrete 3-step method (pick target agencies, pull last 10 awards, trace the vehicle) that any reader can run next week. The community question is engineered for specificity — asking for agency + NAICS code lowers the comment barrier while surfacing real buyer intelligence Josh can publicly engage with, demonstrating authority in the replies. Expected to move **comments** (readers who share their agency targets and invite a direct reply from Josh) and **reactions** (from readers who recognize this as insight they wish they'd had at the start of their vehicle research).
