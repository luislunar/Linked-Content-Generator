"You can't win federal contracts without past performance."

I've heard this so many times I've lost count. And I get it — that requirement shows up everywhere in solicitations. But here's what most people miss: contracting officers evaluate **teams**, not just individual firms.

Teaming agreements exist for exactly this reason. Partner with an established prime as a subcontractor, and their past performance becomes part of the record the CO reviews. The barrier isn't gone — it's borrowed.

Companies that figure this out early stop asking "how do I build federal experience from scratch?" and start asking: "Which primes are already winning work in my space — and what do I bring to the table that makes me worth teaming with?"

That's the right question. It changes your whole approach to GovCon.

The past performance wall is real. But it was never meant to be climbed alone.

If you're holding back from federal contracting because you don't have past performance yet — what specific capability would you bring to a teaming partner?
