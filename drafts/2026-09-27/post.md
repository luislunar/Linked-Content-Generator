When did you last actually audit your GSA Schedule contract?

Not "check that it's active." A real audit.

Having a Schedule doesn't make you compliant. I see this constantly: small businesses earn the Schedule, feel relieved, and move on. Then a contracting officer pulls a price comparison 18 months later — and the discrepancy is a problem nobody saw coming.

Your Schedule isn't a trophy. It's a living contract with ongoing obligations.

Here's the 5-item audit most holders skip:

1. **Basis of Award review** — Has your commercial pricing changed in the last year? Your GSA rates must stay aligned with your current best commercial price.

2. **SAM.gov registration** — When does it expire? A lapsed registration freezes all federal payments. Check the date today.

3. **TAA compliance check** — Are all your offered products and services still Trade Agreements Act-compliant? Country-of-origin rules shift, and so do your offerings.

4. **Quarterly sales reporting** — Have you logged your Schedule sales in the SSQ (Schedule Sales Query) system? It's due every quarter. Missed filings trigger penalties.

5. **Open modifications** — Do you have pending mods sitting idle — updated labor categories, new prices, revised terms? Contracting officers notice.

Most holders find at least one gap the first time they run this.

The good news: each of these is fixable. The risk is not knowing they exist.

Which of these 5 are you already checking? Which one are you adding to your compliance process this week?
