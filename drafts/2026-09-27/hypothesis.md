This draft tests a **question hook on a compliance checklist** — a combination not used in the last two weeks of rotation. The question ("When did you last actually audit your GSA Schedule?") is designed to create mild self-conscious tension in holders who know they haven't done it recently, which typically drives both reactions (recognition) and comments (people sharing their own audit gaps or asking follow-up questions about specific items).

The topic (compliance / Schedule maintenance) targets the overlooked second half of the ICP — small businesses who *already have* a GSA Schedule but aren't actively managing it. This group tends to be highly engaged on LinkedIn because the content directly applies to their situation right now, rather than aspirationally.

The numbered format (1–5 items) breaks from the recent run of `→` bullet and prose-heavy posts, and the dark `single-06-checklist` template fixes two consecutive cream-bg cards in the feed. Expected score movers: **reactions** from the self-recognition hook ("I haven't done this in months"), and **comments** from the community question asking which item they're adding — which invites specific, personal answers rather than vague agreement.
