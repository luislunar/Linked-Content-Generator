This draft tests a **story hook** (client-doubt anecdote) on a **milestone** post anchored to fresh Q3 2026 USAspending.gov data ($21.4B to small businesses). The hypothesis is that leading with a relatable client belief — "only for the big guys" — before revealing the stat creates a tension-and-reveal structure that outperforms a cold stat drop as an opener, because readers in IT, construction, and consulting will recognize themselves in that doubt before the number reframes their assumption. The broad `gsa_basics` topic maximizes feed relevance across the full ICP. The prose format (no bullets) after three recent posts with bullets or lists gives the feed a tonal reset and lets the data carry the weight. Expected lift: **reactions** from owners who share the "too complicated" belief the post names directly, and **comments** from people willing to state their real blocker in response to the specific experience-share CTA — which asks for a named reason rather than a generic opinion.
