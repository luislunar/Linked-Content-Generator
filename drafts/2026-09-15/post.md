"Should I go after GSA MAS or OASIS+?"

Wrong question.

OASIS+ requires deep past performance in complex professional services. CIO-SP4 drew thousands of applicants competing for limited spots. SEWP requires a federal agency to nominate you before you can even apply. These aren't entry points — they're earned positions.

GSA Multiple Award Schedule is the only vehicle with an open application that any eligible small business can pursue on their own timeline. No nomination. No invitation. More than 10,000 federal agencies buy through it across every category of product and service.
