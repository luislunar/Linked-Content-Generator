Most GSA Schedule holders think the hard part is getting on contract.

It's not.

Last week I was reviewing a client's Basis of Award documentation — their commercial sales practices disclosure specifically. Q4 had brought a round of commercial price changes, but no modification had been filed to update their Schedule rates. That's a live compliance gap. Under the price reduction clause, it doesn't have to be intentional to be a problem.

We caught it in a quarterly audit we run inside a shared compliance tracker — a spreadsheet tied to their invoice history and current Schedule pricing. Twenty minutes of review. Modification filed the same day.

Nobody talks about this part of the Schedule because it's not exciting. But this is what ongoing compliance actually looks like: routine catches before they become formal issues.

The Schedule is a living contract. Most people forget that the moment approval day passes.
