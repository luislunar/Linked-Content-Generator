This draft tests a client_story format (prose, no bullets) using a truth hook on the pipeline topic. The hook opens with "Nobody talks enough about..." — a pattern designed to signal insider knowledge and make readers lean in before the specifics arrive. The format is deliberately prose-only: no bullets, no `→` marks, just a tight narrative arc from silence to pipeline activity. The story anchors on a specific and concrete tool (beta.SAM.gov, Sources Sought notices, NAICS search) which gives readers an immediate action to take — or reveals a gap in their current behavior. The before/after transformation (eight months idle → two RFQs in three months) is modest and therefore believable, which is intentional: oversized wins trigger skepticism in this audience. Expected score impact: strong reactions from Schedule holders who recognize the passive-waiting pattern in themselves, and above-average comments from people sharing their own experience (or admitting they've never responded to a Sources Sought notice). The closing question targets that admission specifically — designed to surface embarrassing honesty rather than generic opinions.
