This draft tests a carousel myth-busting format on the `myths` topic using a contrarian story hook. The opener leads with a personal anecdote (first-person client moment) rather than a statement, deliberately breaking the pattern of 3 consecutive statement-led posts. The carousel format is chosen because myths are inherently enumerable and invite swipe-through behavior — each slide delivers a discrete tension (myth) and release (reality), which tends to hold attention longer than a single scroll. The 3 myths selected (past performance, schedule passivity, application cost) map directly to the ICP's most commonly stated objections and should resonate with owners who self-screened out of GovCon before ever consulting a professional. Expected score impact: higher-than-average comments from people sharing which myth they believed, and solid reactions from followers who will tag peers who hold these misconceptions. The community question ("Which one did you believe?") is designed to surface first-person stories rather than opinions, which drives reply quality and thread depth.
