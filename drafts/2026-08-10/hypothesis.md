This draft tests a `blunt` hook pattern on the `sam` topic using a `personal_story` format. The opening line lands a hard fact without softening it ("I wish it did" adds empathy without diluting the bluntness), then the narrative arc builds tension through a specific near-miss scenario before resolving with a simple, repeatable fix.

The SAM.gov lapse angle was chosen because it is a universal fear for anyone building toward federal contracting — it can happen to a well-positioned company doing everything else right. That "it could be me" recognition drives reactions. The community question ("when did you last check?") is designed to prompt honest self-audits in the comments, since most readers won't know their expiration date off the top of their heads. Expected to move: reactions (relatable fear/validation) and comments (experience-sharing and self-disclosure). The closing line — "The Schedule is the key. SAM.gov is the door." — provides a quotable frame that may also generate saves.
