This draft tests the **carousel format on a myths topic** using a **question hook** that triggers immediate self-recognition ("which of these have you heard?"). The three myth/reality pairs are intentionally sequenced to target distinct ICP fear states: Myth 1 addresses the experience barrier (barriers to entry), Myth 2 targets passive-income misconceptions (expectation mismatch among holders who aren't generating revenue), and Myth 3 targets admin complacency (the SAM lapse risk that affects even committed applicants). By covering all three in one swipe experience, at least one slide should be personally relevant to nearly every reader — which should lift saves-per-impression above the single-card baseline. The question CTA in both the caption and the close slide creates two reinforcing comment entry points; the close slide's "drop the myth you believed" prompt is designed to generate two distinct comment cohorts (owners sharing a former barrier vs. owners still at the door), keeping the thread active longer. Expected score movers: **reactions** (recognition moments on each slide) and **comments** (personal myth stories, low friction to answer).
