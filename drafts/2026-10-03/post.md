Before you sign that teaming agreement, ask yourself: did you read the workshare clause?

I've reviewed hundreds of these. The small business brings capability, the prime brings the vehicle and the relationship — but the workshare clause is where the deal actually gets real. A prime opens at 25%. You need 35–40% or it's not worth your bandwidth. That negotiation takes one conversation if you come prepared. Most small businesses aren't.

Here's the part almost nobody catches: the teaming agreement and the subcontract agreement are two different documents. The teaming agreement gets you in the door. The subcontract agreement is where your scope, billing timeline, and IP rights get locked in — or quietly left out. I've seen companies agree to the teaming doc, then receive a subcontract with none of those terms in writing.

Know what you're signing before you sign it.
