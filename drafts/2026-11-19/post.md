Your GSA Schedule pricing isn't just a number. It's a legal commitment that follows you for years.

Here's what most small businesses don't know before they submit:

1. **Your GSA price is a ceiling, not a floor.** You can bid lower for a specific order, but your ceiling is public — and agencies will check it before they engage.

2. **Basis of Award determines everything.** GSA compares your rates to the best price you've given any commercial customer. If that baseline is wrong, everything downstream is wrong.

3. **Price reductions can trigger automatically.** Drop your rate for a commercial client and you may owe GSA the same discount. Many businesses discover this during an audit — not before.

4. **Low prices don't win work. Sustainable prices do.** Agencies want competitive rates, not the cheapest vendor. Undercutting yourself kills margin without winning bids.

5. **Raising prices after approval takes 60–90 days.** A modification to increase your Schedule rates isn't fast. Set the right number before you submit — not after you've lost margin on three contracts.

The best time to get your pricing right is before you apply. The second best time is now.
