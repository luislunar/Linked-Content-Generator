This post tests a `blunt` hook + numbered `checklist` format on the `pricing` topic using the `tips_list` post type. Pricing is a high-anxiety area for GovCon newcomers — they know mistakes here carry long-term consequences, but most don't know the specific tripwires (Basis of Award miscalculation, automatic price-reduction clauses, slow modification timelines). The numbered structure makes each point skimmable and shareable, lowering the activation energy to engage. The community question asks for personal mistake stories, which invites vulnerable, high-signal replies. Expected to move **reactions** (practical, save-worthy content) and **comments** (readers surfacing their own pricing war stories or asking follow-up questions about BOA and price reductions).
