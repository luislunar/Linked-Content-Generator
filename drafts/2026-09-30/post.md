A consultant came to me six months after getting her GSA Schedule.

She had the contract. She had her pricing set. She had a polished capability statement ready to send. And she had no pipeline — because she had never learned to work it.

The Schedule opens the door. It doesn't generate revenue on its own.

Here's what changed when she stopped waiting and started building:

→ She responded to every Sources Sought notice in her NAICS codes — not to win, but to get in front of contracting officers before the formal RFQ dropped.
→ She set up keyword alerts on SAM.gov. Three active recompetes surfaced that she never knew existed.
→ She showed up to one industry day, met two primes looking for subs, and teamed her way into her first federal award eleven months in.

Her pipeline is full now. No cold outreach. No guessing. Just consistent presence in the right places at the right time.

Most small businesses skip these steps because they feel premature. They aren't — they're the whole strategy.

The companies that win on GSA started working the pipeline before they needed it.
