Blunt truth: Most small businesses price their GSA Schedule wrong — before they ever submit.

I've seen it dozens of times. The Schedule gets approved, the prices go live, and then nothing happens. Not because of competition. Because the pricing is broken.

Five mistakes that quietly price you out of federal awards:

1. **Copy-pasting your commercial rate sheet.** GSA pricing is a negotiated contract, not a quote. Your Basis of Award dictates what you must offer the government — and it's tied to your best commercial deal.

2. **Ignoring the Price Reduction Clause.** If you ever discount a commercial client below your GSA price, you're legally required to lower your Schedule rate to match. Most companies don't track this.

3. **Bundling what should be line items.** Contracting officers compare line-by-line. Bundled services become invisible in procurement searches — your quote disappears before a human reads it.

4. **Accepting the CO's first counter-offer.** The contracting officer will push back on your rates. That's the process. Walking away from the table too fast locks in a margin hit you'll carry for years.

5. **Setting outlier rates that vanish in GSA Advantage searches.** COs work within budget targets. Pricing that falls outside the norm gets filtered before your name is ever seen.

Small businesses captured $23.1B in federal awards in Q3 2026 alone (Source · USAspending.gov). Pricing is the self-inflicted barrier between most companies and that pipeline.
