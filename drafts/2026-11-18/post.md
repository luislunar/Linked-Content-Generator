Most people assume renewing their SAM.gov registration is just logging in and clicking "renew."

Before we touch that button, we run three checks. First: NAICS codes against what the client actually delivers today. Codes drift — add a new service line and you stop seeing the RFQs you should be winning. Second: the representations and certifications section, all 40+ questions. Most clients answered these at registration and haven't been back. Some answers are wrong. A CO can and will flag that. Third: EFT routing — the bank details tied to federal payments. A stale account number goes quiet without warning. Award lands, nothing moves. The renewal clicks take 20 minutes. The three-step audit before it takes closer to 2–3 hours. That part is not optional.
