This draft tests the carousel format (last used 17 days ago) on the myths topic using a myth-bust hook. The opening line leads with the single most paralyzing myth in GovCon — past performance — to stop the scroll. The three slides cover the three highest-frequency objections I hear from small businesses: no track record, passive income illusion, and the time-cost calculus. The comparison format (myth vs. reality per slide) is designed to drive saves and shares, since readers will want to forward each slide to someone stuck on the same belief. Expected lift: reactions from readers who recognize themselves in a myth, and comments from people naming the specific myth that almost stopped them — a direct prompt that lowers the barrier to engagement.
