A management consulting firm reached out to us after two years of trying to break into federal contracting on their own.

Strong commercial track record. Good team. No federal revenue. Every attempt at government work hit the same wall: no connections, no past performance, and no clear path to finding the right opportunities.

When they got on the GSA MAS, the first thing we focused on wasn't marketing — it was building a real pipeline process. We walked them through how to use FPDS to find recompetes before they ever hit SAM.gov. How to read a Sources Sought notice, and when it's worth responding. How to map which agencies were already buying in their NAICS code. How to use eBuy to see inbound RFQs arriving in real time.

Six months later: structured pipeline in place, inbound RFQs arriving, first federal proposal submitted. The outreach wasn't cold anymore — they knew who was buying, what they needed, and when contracts were cycling for renewal.

Getting on the Schedule removes the barrier. Building the process to work what's on the other side is what makes it worth the wait.
