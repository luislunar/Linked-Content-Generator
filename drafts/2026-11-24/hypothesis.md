This draft tests the `carousel` format with a `myth-bust` hook on the `myths` topic — a combination that hasn't appeared in the last 5 runs. The carousel structure forces a swipe interaction, which typically lifts impressions and dwell time, while the closing "which myth almost stopped you?" prompt directly targets the ICP's lived experience of hesitation. The three myths chosen (past performance requirement, lowest-price mandate, passive inbound from the catalog) are the most common objections the sales team hears before a prospect disqualifies themselves. Expected to move: **comments** (readers validating their own story) and **reactions** (relief recognition — "I believed that too"). Secondary test: whether the myth-bust frame on a carousel outperforms the same frame on a single-image post, since the story unfolds across slides rather than in one block of text.
