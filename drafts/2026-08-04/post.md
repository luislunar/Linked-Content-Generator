Nobody talks enough about the actual scale of the federal market.

In Q2 2026 alone, small businesses were awarded $14.2 billion in federal contracts — across just 8 industries. IT, engineering, construction, consulting, R&D, facilities, computer services, management consulting.

That's one quarter. Not one year.

The Department of Defense sent $10.9B to small businesses last quarter. The VA added $3.6B. DHS added $3.2B.

These aren't lottery numbers. They're market signals.

The companies winning those awards aren't special. They have the right vehicles in place and they show up consistently when RFQs land.

The ones sitting on the sideline usually share one of three blockers: they don't know if they qualify, the process feels too complicated, or they tried once and got lost in the paperwork.

All three are fixable.
