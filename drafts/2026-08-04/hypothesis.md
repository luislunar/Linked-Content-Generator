This draft tests whether a data-led stat hook on market scale drives higher comment engagement than purely educational content. The hook uses the "truth" pattern — framing fresh Q2 2026 USAspending.gov figures ($14.2B to small businesses in a single quarter across 8 industries) as an uncomfortable reality most readers haven't internalized. By naming specific agencies (DoD $10.9B, VA $3.6B, DHS $3.2B) with real dollar figures, the post makes the abstract market feel concrete and local to the reader's own industry. The all-prose format, without bullets, lets each short paragraph land as its own beat — similar to high-engagement financial commentary on LinkedIn. Expected to move: **reactions** (shareable stat, surprising scale for first-time readers) and **comments** (closing question surfaces authentic blockers like qualification anxiety or prior failed attempts — specific enough to invite real answers, not generic "what do you think" responses). Opt-ins unlikely since no direct service pitch is made; the post positions Josh as a market-data authority rather than selling anything.
