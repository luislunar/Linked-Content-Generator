Wrong assumption I see constantly in GovCon: "Once I get my 8(a) or SDVOSB, the contracts will follow."

They won't. Not automatically.

Certifications shrink the competitive pool — they're a real advantage. But they don't create a mechanism for agencies to pay you. Agencies buy through contract vehicles: a GSA Schedule, SEWP, OASIS+. Without one, your certification sits on your website and goes nowhere.

The certification tells them you qualify. The vehicle is how money actually moves.

If you're certified and not generating federal revenue, ask yourself: which vehicle are you actually visible on?
