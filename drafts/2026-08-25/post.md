Most companies get their GSA Schedule approved and then wait. I've seen it happen more times than I can count.

Week one with a new client, I'm not starting the application. I'm in USAspending.gov pulling what federal agencies paid last quarter in their NAICS categories. I'm mapping recompetes coming up in the next 18 months. I'm building a shortlist of contracting officers who have already written checks to companies doing exactly what my client does.

By the time we file a single page of paperwork, they have a pipeline map. The application is 100+ hours of documentation. The strategy takes an afternoon. Most people skip it entirely — then wonder why the Schedule isn't producing revenue.

The Schedule is a door. You still have to know which rooms are on the other side.
