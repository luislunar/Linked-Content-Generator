A CEO of a $4M IT consulting firm told me she almost didn't apply for a GSA Schedule.

"We're too small," she said. "The government doesn't buy from companies like ours."

I've heard this a hundred times.

Eleven months later, she had her first federal task order. Not luck — she got on the Schedule, then consistently worked the opportunity pipeline the right way.

Before the application: zero federal revenue, no contracting officer relationships, a sales team focused entirely on commercial clients. SAM.gov wasn't even in her vocabulary.

After the Schedule was awarded, three things shifted:
→ She could finally respond to RFQs her firm had been blocked from before
→ Her team had the credibility signal that actually opens doors in federal BD
→ Month eleven: first federal award

The Schedule doesn't hand you contracts. What it does is remove the first and biggest barrier — being eligible.

If your commercial model is solid and your revenue is $2M or more, the question isn't "can we win federal contracts?" The real question is why you're leaving that market to someone else.
