Wrong question: "How do I get my own GSA Schedule before I pursue federal business?"

Here's the right one: which prime already has a Schedule in my category, and what do they need from a sub?

Primes with large GSA contracts are required to meet small business subcontracting goals — it's written into their contract. The VA, DoD, and DHS all mandate it. A qualified sub with a sharp capability statement isn't asking for a favor. They're solving a compliance problem for the prime.

Sub on two or three contracts. Build documented past performance with real federal agencies. Then apply for your own Schedule with actual proof behind you. The contracting officer approving your application won't care that you came in through the side door. They'll care that you showed up with receipts.

Teaming isn't settling. It's the entrance ramp most people drive right past.
