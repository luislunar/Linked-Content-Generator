Your SAM.gov registration is silently working against you.

Most small businesses set it up once, then forget it. The problem: agencies pull your SAM data before every award decision. If anything's stale, you're out before the conversation starts.

Five things vendors forget to update:

1. **NAICS codes** — Wrong or missing codes mean you don't appear in solicitation searches for work you're qualified for.
2. **Point of contact info** — A dead phone number stops a Contracting Officer mid-outreach. They move on.
3. **Exclusions check** — Any entity change (new ownership, address, EIN) triggers re-verification requirements most vendors miss.
4. **CAGE code alignment** — Address mismatches create payment delays even after you win a contract.
5. **Annual renewal date** — Miss it by one day and your entity goes inactive. No grace period. No warning email. Gone.

The barrier to federal work is usually paperwork — not competition.
