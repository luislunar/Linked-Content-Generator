This draft tests the `alert` hook pattern on a `sam` topic using a `tips_list` format rendered on the dark-navy `single-06-checklist` template. The opener — "Your SAM.gov registration is silently working against you" — creates immediate personal urgency for small business owners who set up SAM once and assumed it stayed current. The numbered format (not `→` bullets) varies the visual rhythm from the last two posts and fits the checklist-style template naturally. The five items were chosen for specificity: each names a concrete gap that's easy to overlook and carries a real consequence (missed solicitations, dead outreach, payment delays, entity deactivation). The community question asks readers to self-report their last SAM audit — low-stakes, specific, and honest — which historically generates comments from people who realize they're overdue. Expected impact: above-average reactions from GSA holders who recognize at least one item they've missed, and comments from readers sharing SAM expiration stories or asking follow-up questions about the renewal process.
