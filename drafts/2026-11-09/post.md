Small businesses received $23.4B in federal contracts last quarter.

That's not a year. That's one quarter — Q3 2026 alone.

The number comes straight from USAspending.gov. The industries that captured it: IT services, construction, consulting, engineering. The same sectors as the companies I talk to every day. Companies with $2M in revenue who are still asking whether federal contracting is "worth it."

That money went somewhere. To companies that had the right vehicle, the right registration, and the right positioning. Most had a GSA Schedule. Many had a certification. Almost all had someone who took the paperwork seriously instead of walking away from it.

The barrier has never been the market. The market is real and it is wide open. The barrier is the wall between you and your first federal award — and that wall is bureaucratic, not competitive.

Your private-sector clients are already buying the same services the federal government buys every day. The question isn't whether there's room for you in this market.
