This draft tests the `truth` hook pattern ("Nobody talks about...") applied to a `pricing` story via `client_story` format. The pricing-audit narrative targets the underserved ICP segment: business owners who already have a GSA Schedule but aren't generating federal revenue. Framing the root cause as a pricing failure — not a market failure — is a psychologically softer entry point that lowers defensiveness and opens the door to self-reflection. The before/after card structure reinforces credibility by making the transformation concrete (18% over-market → repriced → first award). Expected: stronger reactions from existing Schedule holders who've experienced a dormant contract, and comment engagement from people sharing their own pricing audit story or asking how to identify where their rates sit relative to competitors on the same SIN.
