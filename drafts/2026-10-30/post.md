Before you apply to a contract vehicle — any contract vehicle — ask yourself: "Can I actually get on this one?"

Most small businesses assume all federal contract vehicles are equally accessible. They're not. Accessibility varies more than most people realize.

Here's the reality check:

1. **GSA MAS** — Self-apply, no invitation needed. 30+ categories covering IT, professional services, construction, health, and more. Open to any eligible small business with two years of commercial revenue.
2. **SEWP & CIO-SP4** — IT-specific and invitation-only. The agencies select who gets on; you cannot nominate yourself.
3. **OASIS+** — Best-in-Class IDIQ. Getting "on" it isn't a free pass — you still compete for every individual task order after award.
4. **8(a) STARS III** — Requires an active SBA 8(a) certification, which is a separate 6–12 month process before you can even apply to the vehicle.
5. **The bottom line** — GSA MAS is the only major multi-agency vehicle you can pursue right now, regardless of certification status or prior federal history.

None of this means GSA Schedule is always the right fit. But for most small businesses trying to enter the federal market, it is the most accessible open door.

Before you choose a vehicle, know what it actually requires to get on it.
