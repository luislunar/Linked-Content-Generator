SAM.gov renews once a year. Most people know that.

What most people miss is the cascade of problems when it lapses — even for a single day.

Active awards freeze. RFQ eligibility disappears. In some cases, incoming payments stop until registration is restored. I've seen it cost companies months of hard-won momentum.

Before your next renewal date, run this audit:

1. **Log in and find your exact expiration date.** Don't assume you know it. Calendar it 60 days out so you're not scrambling at the last minute.
2. **Review your NAICS codes.** Your business has evolved since you first registered. Your primary NAICS should reflect what you're actually bidding and winning — not what you were doing three years ago.
3. **Confirm your banking details match your current account.** Changed banks? Moved to a new business account? SAM needs to reflect that before your next award hits.
4. **Verify your socioeconomic certifications.** WOSB, SDVOSB, 8(a), HUBZone designations live inside your SAM profile. If those flags are stale or expired, you're leaving set-aside opportunities on the table.
5. **Check your E-Verify status.** Federal contractors are required to maintain it. A lapsed E-Verify quietly disqualifies you from new awards without a single warning email.

This whole audit takes under 30 minutes. Skipping it costs a lot more.
