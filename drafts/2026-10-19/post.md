Nobody has "found pipeline." Most people stumble into a Sources Sought notice and call it a strategy.

Here's what actually happens when we build pipeline for a client at GSA Focus.

We start on USAspending.gov. Pull every award in the client's primary NAICS codes for the past two fiscal years. We're looking at ceiling sizes, incumbent names, contract lengths, and modification patterns. That data tells us which agencies actually spend in their space — and which ones just talk about it.

Then we cross-reference beta.SAM.gov and filter for Sources Sought notices. Procurement officers post these before the formal solicitation exists — it's their way of signaling intent before the RFQ drops. Most small businesses never read them. We do. Every week. For every client.

That's the whole system. No secrets. No insider access. A repeatable search protocol applied consistently over time.

Pipeline isn't luck. It's a habit.
