Before you sign a teaming agreement, ask these five questions.

I've seen too many small businesses "team" their way into a dead end — the prime walks away with the award and a thank-you email is all the sub gets to show for 18 months of effort.

Teaming works. But only when you go in with eyes open.

1. **Who's actually bringing the business?**
Confirm the prime has a real, active pipeline — not a plan to find work someday.

2. **Who owns the customer relationship after award?**
That relationship is often worth more than this single contract. Don't let it default to the prime by accident.

3. **Is your scope in writing?**
"We'll figure it out after award" is how good intentions turn into disputes. Teaming agreements need specifics.

4. **What is your exact subcontract percentage?**
"Best efforts to subcontract" is not a number. Get a written percentage before you sign.

5. **Can you use this work as past performance on future proposals?**
Confirm it explicitly. Past performance credit is often the entire reason a new entrant agrees to team in the first place.

One solid teaming deal can unlock your first federal win. One vague one wastes 18 months and leaves no record to show for it.
