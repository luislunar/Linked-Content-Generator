This draft tests a data-anchored contrarian hook on the gsa_basics topic using real Q3 2026 USAspending.gov figures. The hypothesis: leading with a concrete dollar figure ($22.4B) that most small business owners would find surprising — immediately paired with the reframe that this money went to companies like theirs, not just primes — will generate higher reactions than abstract educational posts, because it triggers a "wait, really?" response. The community question is designed to solicit self-identification (industry + awareness of their federal buyer landscape), which should drive comments from owners who have never considered the federal channel and want to learn more. Expected score movers: reactions (curiosity + share instinct from a striking number) and comments (owners naming their industry and gap in awareness).
