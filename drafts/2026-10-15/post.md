Most small businesses try to price their GSA proposal as low as possible.

That's the wrong move.

Your GSA rates aren't your floor — they're your ceiling. Price too low and you've locked yourself into unprofitable work for the life of the contract. Price too high without documentation and the contracting officer pushes back hard.

The goal isn't to be the cheapest line item in the schedule. It's to price in a way that's defensible, sustainable, and competitive.

Pricing wrong on the front end is one of the most expensive mistakes I see. And unlike most mistakes, it's nearly impossible to fix after award.
