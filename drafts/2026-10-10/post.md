Is the federal market really locked up by Booz Allen and Leidos?

Q3 2026 data says otherwise. DHS alone awarded $6.7 billion to small businesses in a single quarter. GSA awarded another $3 billion on top of that. Across the top eight federal industries, small businesses captured $20.7 billion in three months. (Source: USAspending.gov)

The market isn't closed. The real competition isn't with Leidos or Booz Allen — they're winning contracts at ceilings most small businesses will never even bid. The actual barrier is positioning: an active SAM.gov registration, a live GSA Schedule, and a capability statement written in the language contracting officers use.

Companies that show up correctly get found. Companies that don't spend years convinced the game is rigged.

It isn't rigged. It just requires knowing how to play it.
