This draft tests the `truth` hook pattern ("Nobody talks enough about...") against the `teaming` topic, targeting ICP readers who already have a GSA Schedule but haven't converted it into federal revenue. The `client_story` format with a concrete before/after arc (zero footprint → prime bid win in 12 months) is designed to generate high credibility reactions from readers who recognize the pattern in their own situation. The `single-05-beforeafter` template makes the transformation legible at a glance without reading the full caption. The community question ("Did teaming play a role, or did you find another way in?") opens two response paths — those who used teaming and want to validate their experience, and those who took a different route and want to share it — which should lift comments above the checklist-style posts. Expected impact: stronger reactions (aspiration + recognition) and above-average comments from Schedule holders stuck at zero federal revenue.
