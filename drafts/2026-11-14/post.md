They had been in business for eleven years.

Solid IT services firm. Good margins, recurring commercial clients, strong technical team. And zero federal revenue — not because they hadn't tried, but because they'd convinced themselves the door was closed.

Their reasoning: federal agencies want past performance. Past performance comes from winning federal contracts. And you can't win federal contracts without past performance. It's a loop, and they were on the outside of it.

I've heard this exact logic dozens of times. It's one of the most expensive beliefs in GovCon.

What they didn't see was the side door: teaming. We connected them with a prime contractor holding an active GSA MAS contract in a complementary space. The prime brought the vehicle and the past performance record. They brought deep technical capability. The prime won the award. They performed.

That work became their federal past performance. Eighteen months later, they had their own GSA Schedule approved and were responding to direct RFQs independently.

The loop wasn't unbreakable. It just required building the right relationship first.

If you've been sitting outside federal contracting waiting to feel "ready" — what specific thing are you waiting for? Be honest with yourself about whether that bar is real or whether you've set it higher than federal agencies actually require.
