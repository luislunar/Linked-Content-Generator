This draft tests the `story` hook pattern on a `teaming` topic using a client narrative format. The past-performance loop is one of the most common mental blockers I hear from ICP prospects — framing it through a real client's journey (not an abstract explanation) lets readers project themselves into the story and recognize their own reasoning. The before/after template forces the visual transformation to be legible at a glance, which should drive saves from readers who want to reference it later. The community question is intentionally specific ("what specific thing are you waiting for?") rather than generic — designed to surface concrete blockers in comments, which should increase comment depth and quality. Expected score movers: reactions (relatable story triggers emotional resonance) and comments (specific question invites specific answers rather than passive likes).
