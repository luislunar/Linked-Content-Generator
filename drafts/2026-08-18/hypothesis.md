This draft tests the `alert` hook pattern on the `sam` topic using a `hot_take` format in pure prose. The hook names a specific, relatable mistake ("SAM.gov registration = done") that many ICP owners have made personally, then reframes SAM as a compliance floor rather than a market strategy. This framing is intentionally contrarian to the assumption most new entrants carry.

The hypothesis: naming a mistake the reader has almost certainly made (or is currently making) will generate higher comment volume than educational posts, because it invites people to share their own "I thought that too" experience or to defend their approach. The community question is designed to surface that exact experience — "longest to figure out" lowers the barrier to share and invites honest reflection rather than a quick reaction. Expected lift: reactions (recognition factor) and comments (story-sharing from owners who waited too long on SAM alone).
