A client called me three years ago convinced their business was too small for federal contracts.

They ran a $4M IT consulting firm — solid commercial revenue, proven capabilities, real clients. But the federal market felt like a locked room. No past performance. No agency relationships. No idea how to get in front of a contracting officer.

Here's what actually happened.

We got them on the GSA Multiple Award Schedule first. Not to generate revenue immediately — but to give them legitimacy as a federal contractor. Then we identified three established primes who needed their exact capabilities as a subcontractor on a DHS vehicle.

Month seven: named sub on a teaming agreement. Month eleven: first federal award hit.

The thing nobody tells you: past performance isn't a prerequisite for teaming — it's the product of it. You earn it by doing the work, not by waiting until you look ready.

CPARS — the federal contractor performance database — builds your reputation over time. As a sub, you're doing real federal work, real reporting, and your company name starts appearing in systems that contracting officers actually search.

Most small businesses wait until they feel competitive. That feeling never comes from the outside. It comes from being inside.
