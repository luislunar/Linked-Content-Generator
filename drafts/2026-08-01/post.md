Everyone asks: "Which contract vehicle is right for my company — GSA MAS, OASIS+, CIO-SP4, SEWP?"

Wrong question.

The real question is: are you positioned to actually win on any vehicle at all?

I've watched businesses spend 18 months debating OASIS+ vs. SEWP while their SAM.gov registration quietly lapsed. I've seen companies obsess over vehicle eligibility while they've never once had a real conversation with a contracting officer.

The vehicle doesn't win contracts. Your positioning, your relationships, and your pipeline discipline do.

GSA MAS is the right starting point for most small businesses — not because it beats every other vehicle on paper, but because it's the most accessible on-ramp to the federal market. It opens the door. What you do on the other side is on you.

The best contract vehicle is the one you actually use.
