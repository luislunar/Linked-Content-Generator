The hook deliberately starts with the question the ICP is already thinking ("Which vehicle is right for me?") and immediately pivots with "Wrong question." This creates a pattern interrupt designed to halt the scroll. The contrarian structure has shown strong reaction rates in past field-notes style posts because it makes readers feel the insight was written specifically for them.

**What's being tested:** Whether a sub-150-word prose inspiration post on the vehicles topic outperforms recent longer-form checklist/numbered content. The vehicles topic tends to attract ICP members actively researching entry points, so a reframe that challenges the vehicle-first mentality could drive comments from people who've gone through the same debate.

**Expected score movement:** Reactions should rise (contrarian tension is shareable), and comments may spike because the closing question — "what's the one thing actually holding you back?" — invites honest self-disclosure rather than a generic "what do you think?" prompt.
