This draft tests the `alert` hook pattern in a `behind_scenes` format on the `pipeline` topic — a combination not run in recent history. The hook opens with a counterintuitive structural problem (holders missing recompetes they should win) rather than a tip or myth, which creates friction that drives engagement. The mention of real, named tools (beta.SAM.gov, usaspending.gov, "recompete calendar") gives the post credibility and specificity that generic pipeline advice lacks. The prose format with no bullets breaks the visual rhythm from recent checklist-heavy posts. Expected lift: reactions from holders who recognize the pain ("this is me"), and comments from people sharing their own monitoring process — or admitting they don't have one. The community question is deliberately uncomfortable in a productive way, targeting the gap between knowing pipeline discipline matters and actually doing it.
