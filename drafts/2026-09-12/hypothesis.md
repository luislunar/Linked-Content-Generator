This draft tests a `truth` hook pattern on the `pipeline` topic using `hot_take` format — a combination not used together in the recent rotation. The core tension is the gap between how most small businesses think pipeline works (reactive bid-chasing) versus how it actually works (relationship-building 6–18 months before an RFP surfaces). The "uncomfortable reality" angle tends to generate comments from people who've experienced this firsthand — either agreeing with frustration or sharing how they changed their approach. The community question is targeted at action-deferral ("the one agency you've been putting off"), which personalizes the challenge and invites responses grounded in real situations rather than general agreement. Expecting above-average comments relative to reactions, since the insight is counterintuitive enough to spark debate but practical enough to be immediately relatable.
