Nobody talks enough about how the federal pipeline actually works.

Most small businesses treat GovCon like a bidding site. They find an RFP on SAM.gov, check the requirements, and start writing. Here's the problem: if you found it the day it posted, you're almost certainly too late.

The contract was shaped 6–18 months before the solicitation dropped. The program office already knows who they like. The scope of work reflects conversations they've already had with vendors who were paying attention.

The companies winning recompetes aren't the cheapest bidders. They're the ones who were at the industry day, responded to the sources sought, and walked the CO through their capabilities before there was a public RFP to chase.

Your pipeline isn't a list of opportunities to bid on. It's a map of agencies you're already known to.

What's the one agency you've been meaning to build a relationship with — and keep putting off?
