This draft tests the `story` hook pattern — opening in media res on a client's situation ("They had everything...") rather than a statement or question — combined with a `teaming` topic that hasn't appeared in the feed for at least five days. The `client_story` post type was chosen because personal_story, tips_list, and hot_take all ran in the previous three days; client_story provides social proof and credibility without repeating any recent type. The prose-only format (no bullets) serves the 40% non-bullet rotation and suits the narrative arc of the before/after transformation. The concrete timeline details — DoD IT support award, six months to first citation, twelve months to GSA approval — are the specifics most likely to generate reactions from owners who recognize themselves in the "wall" moment. The closing CTA is deliberately binary (right prime vs. structuring the agreement), which tends to lower the friction for commenters to pick a side and respond with something specific rather than a vague "great post."
