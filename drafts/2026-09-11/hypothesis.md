This draft tests a `question` hook on a `client_story` format in the `certifications` topic — a combination not run in the last 10 days. The "Before you..." opener creates a direct challenge to a common reader assumption (that getting certified is the hard part), which should drive pause-and-read behavior. The story structure — specific details (IT consulting, 18 months, WOSB), a named failure mode (no transactional vehicle), and a concrete outcome (month 11 award) — is designed to score high on comments because it surfaces a real gap that certified-but-stalled business owners will recognize and want to share their own version of. The closing question is experience-share format ("what gap have you run into") rather than opinion, which tends to draw more personal responses. Expected movement: reactions from WOSB/certified owners who feel seen, comments from people sharing their own cert-without-vehicle story or asking how to solve it.
