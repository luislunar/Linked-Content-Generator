This draft tests whether a data-led stat post with a blunt hook drives stronger engagement than the recent narrative/checklist formats. The hypothesis: opening with a specific, striking figure ($15.7B to small businesses in one quarter) immediately challenges the dominant belief that federal contracting is inaccessible to small firms. By naming the myth directly ("the federal market belongs to Booz Allen") before presenting the counter-evidence, the post creates a tension-release that should boost reactions from ICP owners who share the frustration. The closing community question — asking what's holding them back and for how long — is deliberately specific and slightly uncomfortable, designed to drive higher-quality comments over generic responses. Expected impact: reactions up (relatable conviction + fresh data), comments up (specific question surfaces genuine blockers the audience will want to share).
