$15.7B in federal contracts went to small businesses in Q2 2026 — across the top 8 industry categories alone.

I say this not to impress you. I say it because most owners I talk to still believe the federal market belongs to Booz Allen, Leidos, and the major primes. It doesn't. DoD sent $15.3B to small businesses that quarter. The VA added $3.6B. GSA alone routed $1.6B to small firms through its contract vehicles.

The money is moving. Consistently. To companies your size.

The barrier isn't the market — it's readiness. A GSA Schedule puts your business onto a contract vehicle that agencies use to buy without running a full procurement cycle. It doesn't guarantee you a dollar; nothing does. But it removes the single biggest structural obstacle between your services and the agencies writing those checks.

The opportunity isn't the problem. Getting positioned to receive it is.
