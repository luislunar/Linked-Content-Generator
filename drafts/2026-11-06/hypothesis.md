The `prose` format with no bullets is chosen deliberately after three consecutive posts used structured formats (checklist, story beats, bullets). Unbroken paragraphs with short sentences create a more personal, conversational cadence — consistent with the "casual, authentic" expectation for `behind_scenes` type posts.

The closing community question ("Not just renew it — actually read it") doubles as a soft challenge and an experience-share prompt. The expected lift is primarily in **comments** from people who either admit they haven't reviewed it in years, or share the specific landmine they hit. Reactions should follow from the relatable framing of a universal mistake.
