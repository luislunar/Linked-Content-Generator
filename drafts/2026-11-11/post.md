Nobody talks enough about what happens to your GSA Schedule after you get it.

The award feels like the finish line. It isn't. It's the starting line of a contract performance obligation — and most small businesses have no idea what that means until something goes wrong.

Your Schedule is a live federal contract. It doesn't just sit there. Mass modifications from GSA land every 6–12 months and require active responses. The Price Reduction Clause can retroactively trigger discounts if your commercial pricing ever drops below what you quoted. Trade Agreements Act compliance applies to every product or service you add, not just at award. Annual certifications aren't optional reminders — they're contract requirements.

I've watched contracts get administratively closed because a holder missed a mass mod response. Three years of hard work to get the Schedule, gone.

Getting your Schedule approved is not the milestone — keeping it compliant is.
