SAM.gov doesn't send you a reminder when your registration expires. Neither does your contracting officer.

If your registration lapses, agencies can't obligate payment to you — even on contracts you've already won.

Here are 5 SAM.gov mistakes I see costing small businesses every month:

1. **Ignoring the expiration date.** Registrations last exactly 12 months. Set a calendar reminder 60 days out — not 7 days.

2. **Listing too few NAICS codes.** Contracting officers search by NAICS code. If your service isn't listed, you don't exist in their system — even if you're fully capable of doing the work.

3. **Stale points of contact.** Old email addresses mean bid invitations never reach the right person. A contract you never heard about is a contract you can't win.

4. **Skipping the annual reps & certs review.** The representations and certifications section auto-rolls forward each year — but regulations update. An unchecked box in the wrong place creates a compliance flag down the road.

5. **Outdated banking information.** I've seen award payments delayed weeks because the direct deposit info pointed to an account the business had closed a year earlier.

None of these are hard to fix. All of them are easy to overlook.
