This draft tests the `alert` hook pattern applied to a `sam` topic using a `tips_list` format with numbered items (no `→` arrows, breaking the recent streak). The hypothesis: listing 5 concrete, commonly missed SAM.gov mistakes will drive reactions from business owners who recognize a past or current error — especially the silent expiration and outdated banking info, which have an "I didn't know that" quality. The community question ("Be honest. 😅") invites candid admissions rather than generic responses, which typically produce more thread engagement. Expected movement: reactions (recognition of relatable mistakes) and comments (people owning up to a lapse or sharing a close call story).
