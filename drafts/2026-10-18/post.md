Nobody talks enough about teaming agreements.

They're the fastest legal path into federal contracting when you don't have past performance yet. And most small businesses skip them entirely because they assume they have nothing to offer a prime.

They do. Here's how to make teaming work:

1. **Match the prime's NAICS code.** Your value isn't your contract history — it's capacity, bandwidth, or a clearance they don't have in-house. Find the prime who needs what you already have.

2. **Look at mentor-protégé programs.** SBA and DoD run structured arrangements that come with guaranteed subcontract work built in. This isn't charity — it's a designed entry ramp.

3. **Get the agreement in writing before the bid drops.** Verbal handshakes don't survive contract award. A signed teaming agreement is the only kind that counts.

4. **Define your work share as a number.** "A meaningful portion" is how disputes start, not how contracts get executed. Lock in the percentage before you submit.

5. **Hunt sources sought notices on SAM.gov.** That's when primes are actively building their teams — before the RFQ is even posted. By the time the formal solicitation drops, the slots are often filled.

If the only thing stopping you from federal work is "no past performance," teaming is your answer. The pathway was built for exactly this situation.
