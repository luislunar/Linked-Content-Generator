This draft tests the `truth` hook pattern ("Nobody talks enough about X") applied to the `teaming` topic, formatted as a numbered tips_list with prose framing rather than bare bullets. The hypothesis is that leading with an uncomfortable truth — that small businesses assume they have nothing to offer a prime, which is incorrect — creates immediate tension that keeps readers through five specific, actionable steps. The numbered structure (1–5) is easier to scan on mobile and lower-friction than `→` bullets, which appeared in 100% of recent posts. The community question targets experience-sharing specifically, which should lift comments from readers who have either tried teaming or been stuck on the past-performance problem. Expected impact: above-average reactions from readers who feel seen on the "no past performance" objection, and 1–3 comments from founders who have navigated a teaming arrangement or want to share what blocked them.
