A Texas construction firm came to us after two years of trying to break into federal contracting. Same wall every time: no past performance.

Every solicitation they found had a past performance requirement. Every prime they reached out to ghosted them. They were ready to quit.

Here's what changed: instead of chasing prime contracts solo, we helped them find a teaming partner who already held a GSA Schedule and had the right past performance in commercial construction.

They came on as a sub on a $1.2M GSA-awarded renovation project at a VA facility. That single job became their past performance. Twelve months later, they had enough track record to pursue their own Schedule application.

Today they're a prime on two active task orders.

Teaming isn't a shortcut. It's the actual path to market for companies with no federal history. The government wants small businesses to win contracts — but it needs proof you can deliver. Teaming is how you build that proof.

You don't have to earn your way in alone.

If you've been stuck on the past performance problem — have you explored teaming arrangements, or have you mostly been trying to win prime contracts on your own first?
