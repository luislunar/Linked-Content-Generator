This draft tests a data-anchored truth hook on the myths topic using an inspiration format. The fresh USAspending.gov figure ($21.6B to small businesses in Q2 2026) makes the federal opportunity feel concrete and verifiable — not a motivational abstraction — which should trigger both surprise reactions from people who underestimated the market size and validation reactions from those already in it. The closing question ("what's made it feel out of reach?") is designed to surface real objections in the comments, targeting the ICP's emotional hesitation rather than a tactical question. Expected lift: reactions (surprise at the dollar figure) and comments (people naming their actual barrier, which is high-value signal for future content).
