This draft tests a **blunt** hook on the **pricing** topic using the `hot_take` format — a single punchy claim (the GSA price list as a legal ceiling) followed by analytical prose that builds the case. The Basis of Award angle is genuinely counter-intuitive: most applicants assume the application is the hard part, not the pricing negotiation, so the opener should create immediate cognitive friction. I chose prose over bullets because `hot_take` posts land harder without the visual interruption — the argument should read as one continuous conviction, not a list. I expect this to drive **reactions** from veterans who've lived through a painful pricing review (recognition/validation) and **comments** from owners currently in the process (anxiety → question-asking), which are the two highest-value engagement types. The "90 days in pricing review" specificity is concrete enough to feel credible without overpromising any outcome.
