When did you last open your SAM.gov registration and actually review it? Be honest. 😅

Most small businesses file their SAM.gov registration once, get their UEI, and never look back. That works — until it doesn't. Your registration is a live document. An expired date, a wrong code, or a stale email address can make you invisible to contracting officers at exactly the wrong moment.

Five things to audit right now:

1. **Expiration date** — SAM.gov requires annual renewal. A lapsed registration freezes payments on every active contract you hold and disqualifies you from new awards.
2. **NAICS codes** — Do your codes reflect what you actually sell today? Wrong codes mean your company won't appear in the right set-aside searches.
3. **Points of contact** — Contracting officers use these to reach you. Stale email addresses mean missed solicitations and no-bid opportunities.
4. **Banking and EFT information** — One wrong routing digit delays payments indefinitely. Verify this any time you change banks.
5. **Exclusions check** — Confirm your company is not flagged in the System for Award Management exclusions database. Most companies that have a problem here don't know until a CO catches it.

A 20-minute audit today can prevent a five-figure payment delay next quarter.

Which of these five have you actually verified this year — or is your SAM.gov running on autopilot?
