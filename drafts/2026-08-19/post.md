Most small businesses think GSA pricing is simple: submit what you charge commercial clients and call it a day.

That's not how it works.

Here's what actually locks in — or limits — your competitiveness on Schedule:

1. **Basis of Award** — GSA ties your rates to your most-favored commercial customer. Define that relationship wrong and you're bound to a discount structure for the life of the contract.
2. **Price reduction clause** — If you lower your commercial rates after award, your GSA prices follow automatically. Most applicants don't know this clause exists until it costs them.
3. **Labor category precision** — Vague SIN descriptions mean agencies can't find you in search results. How you define your services determines visibility, not just compliance.
4. **Pre-submission benchmarking** — Check what approved vendors in your SIN are already charging before you submit. Walking in blind means walking in uncompetitive.

Getting on Schedule gets you in the room. Getting on Schedule at rates you can actually win with — that's what keeps you there.
