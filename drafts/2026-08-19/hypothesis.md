This draft tests whether a myth-bust carousel format on the `myths` topic drives meaningfully higher comment volume than recent single-card posts. The three myths chosen (past performance requirement, guaranteed pipeline, and 6-week timeline) each represent a belief the ICP holds when ruling themselves out before applying — making each slide a potential self-recognition moment. The `close` slide is designed to convert that recognition into a comment ("drop the myth you believed longest"), which is a stronger prompt than a generic opinion question. Expected movement: comments (readers self-identify with one myth) and reactions (carousel novelty + relatable content). The cover headline "3 myths that block your federal entry" is direct enough to earn the swipe without overselling the content.
