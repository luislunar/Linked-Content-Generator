# Hypothesis — 2026-08-30

This draft tests whether a **contrarian hook** ("Wrong question.") on a **pricing** topic outperforms the recent run of alert and story openers in driving comments. The client_story format anchors credibility with a concrete, specific detail (a 12% rate revision) — more believable than a generic transformation claim. The central insight (Basis of Award locks in a floor, not a ceiling) is counterintuitive to most small business owners who think of GSA pricing as a rate card they can adjust later. That gap between their mental model and reality is the tension that should drive reactions. The community question is practitioner-targeted ("if you've been through negotiations") rather than open to all — this should attract fewer but higher-quality comments from people with real experience, which moves the comment score component more than volume alone.
