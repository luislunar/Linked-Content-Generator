"Getting certified means you'll win government contracts."

I've heard this from hundreds of small business owners. And I understand why — earning 8(a), WOSB, SDVOSB, or HUBZone status takes real work. It feels like crossing a finish line.

Here's what's actually true: certifications unlock set-aside doors that uncertified companies can't enter. That part is real and worth pursuing.

But the certification is the key. It isn't the door-knock.

Winning set-aside work still requires a proper GSA Schedule, an active pipeline, and relationships with contracting officers. The businesses getting awards have all three — not just the badge.

The certification gets you in the right race. Winning it is still on you.

If you have a certification, what was the first real door it opened for you — and what did it actually take to walk through?
