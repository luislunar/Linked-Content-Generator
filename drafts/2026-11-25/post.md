A prospect told me last week that the federal government "doesn't really work with companies their size."

I pulled up USAspending.gov and showed them what happened in Q3 2026.

$23.4 billion. Awarded to small businesses in a single quarter — spread across IT services, construction, management consulting, engineering, and healthcare. Not flowing to Lockheed or Booz Allen. To companies doing $2M, $8M, $20M in commercial revenue. The same profile as the person sitting across from me.

The federal market isn't closed to small businesses. The paperwork just makes it feel that way. Once you have the right vehicle in place — a GSA Schedule, a SEWP slot, a teaming agreement — that money has a path to your door.

The barrier isn't the market size. It never was.
