This draft tests a data-reveal story hook on a `gsa_basics` topic using a real Q3 2026 USAspending.gov figure ($23.4B to small businesses in one quarter). The hypothesis is that leading with a first-person skeptic encounter — then dropping a verifiable big number — creates higher emotional engagement than a straight stat post, because readers see themselves in the prospect's position. The `single-04-stat` card amplifies the number visually, reinforcing credibility. The community question targets experience-shares ("what made you believe?") rather than opinions, which should drive comments from people who've crossed the same credibility threshold. Expected lift: reactions (relatable moment) and comments (personal conviction stories).
