One pricing mistake during your GSA application will follow you for the next 5 years.

Most applicants focus on getting approved. Nobody warns them about what happens after.

When you negotiate your GSA price, you're setting a ceiling you'll compete from for years. Price too high — contracting officers search the schedule before every RFQ, and expensive listings get skipped. Price too low — you've locked yourself into margins that won't sustain the overhead of federal work.

The Basis of Award clause makes it worse. You must offer the government the same discount you've given your most-favored commercial client. That sounds fair — until you remember every informal rate cut, every "we'll make an exception this time," every promotional deal you've ever handed out. Those are all on the table now.

I've seen companies complete the entire application process, get approved, enter the marketplace, and generate nothing. Not because agencies weren't buying. Because their price made them invisible in search results.

Before you submit: pull every commercial discount you've offered in writing. Then search GSA Advantage for what your competitors are actually charging.

Approval is the starting line. Your price is the race.
