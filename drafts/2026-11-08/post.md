Nobody talks about this part of the GSA Schedule.

Getting approved feels like the finish line. It isn't.

The Schedule puts you in front of federal buyers — contracting officers, procurement teams, agencies with real budgets. But it doesn't close the sale. It doesn't send follow-ups. It doesn't remind a CO you exist.

I've watched small businesses spend 8–12 months earning their Schedule, then wait for the phone to ring.

It doesn't ring.

The holders who win contracts aren't always the most qualified. They're the most consistent. They show up at industry days. They respond to sources sought notices. They track recompetes 18 months out and get in front of the buyer before the RFP drops.

The Schedule is a hunting license. The hunt is still yours.

If you have a GSA Schedule right now — what does your outreach cadence actually look like? Be honest.
