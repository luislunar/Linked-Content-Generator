Before you submit another proposal without a certification advantage — read this.

Not all small business certifications are equal. Each one opens a different set-aside pool, and the wrong one wastes 6–12 months of prep.

Here's the breakdown:

1. **8(a) Business Development** — 9-year program for socially and economically disadvantaged firms. Sole-source awards up to $4.5M (services) with no competition required. The single biggest unlock in federal contracting for qualifying businesses.

2. **WOSB / EDWOSB** — Women-Owned Small Business and Economically Disadvantaged WOSB. Opens set-asides in industries where women are statistically underrepresented. Two eligibility tiers — verify which designation fits your revenue before you apply.

3. **SDVOSB / VOSB** — Service-Disabled Veteran-Owned and Veteran-Owned. Critical detail: VA certifications now run through VetCert, not SAM.gov. One missed step here can quietly freeze your entire VA pipeline.

4. **HUBZone** — Historically Underutilized Business Zone. Your principal office AND 35% of employees must be in a qualifying zone. The address requirement is where most HUBZone applications fall apart — before they even start.

5. **SBA Small Business (default)** — If you meet size standards, you're already certified. But verify your NAICS codes. One wrong size standard can disqualify a bid without a single conversation with a Contracting Officer.

One certification can unlock thousands of set-aside contracts. But you need to know which ones you actually qualify for before you start the clock.
