"You need government contracts before you can qualify for the GSA Schedule."

I hear this at least three times a week. It's wrong every single time.

GSA Schedule eligibility is built on your *commercial* track record — not your federal history. Two years in business, documented commercial revenue, financials that hold up. That's the actual bar.

The Schedule exists specifically to bring qualified small businesses into the federal market. It's the door. Not the trophy room you earn after you're already inside.

Federal past performance matters when you're bidding on specific contracts. It has nothing to do with whether you can get on the Schedule in the first place.

This one myth keeps hundreds of qualified companies from even starting the conversation.
