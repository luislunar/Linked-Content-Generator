This draft tests a `question` hook on a `tips_list` format covering `pricing` — specifically the ongoing compliance obligations that most GSA Schedule holders don't actively monitor. The hypothesis: framing the post as a pre-bid audit checklist (rather than a "how to get a Schedule" explainer) targets an under-served segment: companies that already have a Schedule but haven't done maintenance. This audience is more likely to engage because the content is immediately actionable and likely to surface recognition ("I haven't checked this in months"). Expected to move **comments** from practitioners sharing which item they've missed or recently caught, and **reactions** from Schedule holders who feel the relevance of the audit framing — particularly on the Price Reduction Clause and EPA clause items, which are commonly misunderstood.
