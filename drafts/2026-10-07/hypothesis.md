This draft tests the `truth` hook pattern on a `client_story` format covering `compliance` — a topic that rarely gets direct treatment but creates quiet anxiety among Schedule holders who've never won a contract. The hypothesis: framing the story as an audit-reveals-silence case (not a how-to compliance checklist) grounds the stakes in a real outcome rather than abstract warnings, which should out-perform generic compliance tips on both reactions and comments. The opening hook ("Nobody talks enough about the companies that have a GSA Schedule — but can't actually use it") targets readers who already hold a Schedule with zero revenue — a segment that may not realize their problem is structural, not strategic. Expected to move **reactions** from holders who recognize their own situation and **comments** from experienced govcon practitioners who've encountered TAA or Option Period issues firsthand.
