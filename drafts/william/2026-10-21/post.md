A question I get on almost every discovery call:

"How do you actually *find* federal opportunities?"

It sounds basic. But it's the right question to ask early — and most small business owners haven't had anyone walk them through a real answer.

Here's the checklist I go through with new prospects:

→ **Sources Sought notices** — These drop before the RFP. Agencies are doing market research, and a solid response puts your name on their radar months ahead of award.

→ **Recompete tracking** — Look at contracts expiring in your NAICS on USAspending.gov. Ending contracts are predictable. Set up alerts and work them.

→ **Industry Days** — When agencies host them ahead of major solicitations, contracting officers notice who shows up. The relationships you build there aren't something you can manufacture later.

→ **eBuy and GSA Advantage** — If you hold a GSA Schedule, RFQs land in these platforms daily. Without the Schedule, most of those doors stay closed.

→ **Agency procurement forecasts** — Nearly every civilian agency publishes what they plan to buy each fiscal year. Most firms I talk to have never read one.

The pipeline isn't hidden. It's just in places most companies haven't thought to look.
