I had a call last week with a WOSB-certified owner who'd spent 18 months on her certification — and still hadn't seen a single federal dollar.

She wasn't doing anything wrong. She just had the sequence backwards.

It's one of the most common patterns I hear on first calls: the certification came first. The contract vehicle never followed.

Here's what the sequence looks like when it actually works:

→ GSA Schedule approved — gives you the vehicle to bid on federal contracts
→ Certification (WOSB, 8(a), SDVOSB) — filters you into set-aside competitions
→ Cert + Schedule together — you're competing in a smaller pool with a real credential

The certification is a multiplier. But you need something to multiply it against.

An 8(a) without a contract vehicle is like having a VIP pass to a venue you can't find.

What I notice on the calls that go somewhere fast: those owners had the Schedule in place — or were actively building it — before they layered on the certification. The ones who feel like "my cert hasn't done anything" almost always went cert-first, vehicle-never.

The cert is worth every step. Just don't let it be the only step.
