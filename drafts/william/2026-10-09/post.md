Something I come back to on almost every first call, because almost every first call needs it:

The business owner assumes the federal market belongs to Booz Allen. To Leidos. To the companies with decades of past performance.

Here's what I tell them.

Federal law mandates that 23% of contract dollars go to small businesses. That's not charity — agencies are graded on hitting that number.

When you're on the GSA Schedule, you're not buried at the bottom of a list next to billion-dollar companies. You're in the set-aside pool. Competing against firms your size, for dollars specifically directed your way.

The owners who call me saying "we're probably too small for this" are often the exact profile a contracting officer is looking for.

The door isn't closed. It's just not labeled the way you'd expect.
