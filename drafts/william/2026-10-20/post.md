Nobody on their first call asks me what happens AFTER approval.

But by month eight — when the paperwork is done and the Schedule is live — that's all anyone wants to know.

Here's what I walk through with every new holder:

→ Your Schedule isn't a one-time filing. It renews every five years — and requires annual representations in SAM.gov. Miss the SAM renewal and your active Schedule goes on hold.

→ Modifications happen more than people expect. Price changes, new SINs, adding a NAICS code — each one goes back to GSA for review. Not a quick edit.

→ Trade Agreements Act (TAA) compliance is ongoing. If you offer products, your supply chain has to stay compliant. One sourcing change can open a gap.

None of this is hard. But it catches people who thought approval was the finish line.

The Schedule is a credential you maintain, not a contract you sign once.
