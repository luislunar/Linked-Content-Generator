This draft tests whether a conversational "first-call FAQ" post — framed as William answering the question he hears most — outperforms posts that lead with a story arc or a bold truth. The question hook ("Does my company even qualify?") is a direct quote from calls, which should create immediate recognition for ICP owners sitting in exactly that mental state. The five-bullet qualification checklist keeps the post educational and shareable without reading like a formal guide, because each bullet is filtered through William's call-observation voice ("Here's what I actually walk through..."). Expected score movers: reactions from owners who've had the same question and feel seen, and comments from people sharing what specific barrier made them hesitate — the CTA is designed to surface that second category. The personal_story format at ~220 words respects the length-variation cadence while staying comfortably within the range.
