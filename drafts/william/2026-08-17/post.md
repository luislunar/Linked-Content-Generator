I hear this on almost every first call with a business that has zero federal contracts:

"Teaming sounds good in theory. But are we just going to be someone else's sub forever?"

It's a fair question. And the answer depends entirely on how you approach it.

Here's what actually moves the needle:

→ Your warm list already exists. Look at your current commercial clients and vendors — some of them are federal primes. That's where to start, not cold outreach.

→ You're solving their mandate, not asking for a favor. Federal primes are contractually required to subcontract a share of their work to small businesses. Find them before the solicitation drops.

→ Get it in writing. "We'll work together on the next one" is a promise, not a teaming agreement. A signed document is what actually shows up in your past performance record.

→ Log every dollar of sub work you complete. Agency, dollar amount, deliverable. That file is what you bring to your own prime bid two or three years from now.

The businesses that go from sub to prime aren't luckier — they documented everything along the way.

If you've explored teaming, what's been the sticking point — finding the right prime, or getting the paperwork formalized?
