Nobody talks about when a GSA Schedule doesn't make sense.

That's usually not how the conversation starts. But it's one of the first things I work through on a first call — not because I'm trying to talk anyone out of it, but because the timing actually matters.

Here's what I look for:

→ You're 2+ years in business. This is a hard requirement. No exceptions.

→ You have commercial clients to point to. The government wants proof you've delivered — not just that you plan to.

→ There's federal spending in your space. A Schedule without a market to sell into is a dormant contract.

→ You have bandwidth to actually use it. The Schedule won't generate leads on its own. If your team can't follow up on RFQs or visit contracting offices, the approval sits idle.

If all four are true? The timing conversation is usually short. If even one isn't — that's usually where we spend the most time.

A lot of owners come to us thinking the question is "do I qualify?" The harder question is often "am I ready to actually use it?"
