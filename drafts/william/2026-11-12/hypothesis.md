This draft tests whether a truth-hook hot_take that reframes the core eligibility question — from "do I qualify?" to "am I ready to actually use it?" — produces higher comment engagement than a standard eligibility checklist. The four-criteria readiness frame (years in business, commercial proof, federal market presence, active bandwidth) is designed to surface recognition in two distinct cohorts: owners who got a Schedule approved and watched it sit idle (who will comment with their version of the wake-up moment), and prospects currently evaluating timing who have one criterion missing (who will comment with their specific blocker). The truth hook opening ("nobody talks about when it doesn't make sense") is expected to move reactions by signaling intellectual honesty rather than a pure sales pitch, increasing trust with both cohorts. The closing reframe ("the harder question is 'am I ready to use it?'") reinforces the central message and invites the CTA to feel like a natural continuation of the internal monologue the reader was already having.
