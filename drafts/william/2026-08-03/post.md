Before you start your GSA Schedule application — when was the last time you actually logged into SAM.gov?

I ask because it's one of the first things I walk through on a call. And a surprising number of business owners haven't touched their SAM profile in months, sometimes longer.

Here's what I find most often:

→ **Registration expired.** SAM requires an annual renewal. Miss the window and your profile goes inactive — which freezes federal payments and disqualifies you from awards automatically.

→ **Wrong or missing NAICS codes.** If your primary NAICS doesn't match what you actually do, you're invisible to the contract searches that matter most.

→ **Entity name mismatch.** The legal entity name in SAM has to align with your UEI exactly. A DBA or old trade name creates a paperwork tangle that slows everything down.

→ **No government point of contact.** If a CO needs to reach you and there's no POC listed, they move on to the next vendor. It takes two minutes to fix.

→ **Reps & Certs never revisited.** Your representations and certifications tell the government who you are. If they haven't been reviewed since you first registered, there's a real chance something is out of date.

None of these are hard to fix — most take under an hour once you know what you're looking at.

But they can quietly block you from every opportunity you're working toward.

When did you last log into SAM.gov to check your profile? What's one thing you found that surprised you?
