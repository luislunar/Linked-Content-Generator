This draft tests whether a practical "before you apply" SAM audit checklist — opened with a direct question grounded in call observations — can drive higher comment volume than William's recent narrative-heavy posts. The five-item checklist format is built to trigger recognition ("that's exactly my situation") among business owners who registered in SAM years ago and haven't logged back in, which is a common call discovery. Expected gains: reactions from the relatable call-out structure (most small business owners can identify with at least one item), and comments from the two-part CTA asking both when they last checked and what they found — a format designed to pull specific, high-signal replies rather than generic agreement.
