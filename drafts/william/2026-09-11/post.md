A prospect called me last week — 11 years in business, solid commercial client base, great reputation.

She'd built her pricing around what the market would bear. It worked.

Then she asked: "Do I just put my standard rates on the GSA Schedule?"

The answer is more complicated than most people expect.

→ Your GSA price isn't just a number. It's tied to a clause called Basis of Award. If you offer a better price to a commercial customer after you're on the Schedule, the government can ask you to match that lower price on your federal contract.

→ Most first-time applicants don't know this going in. They quote what feels right, get approved, then realize their pricing isn't as flexible as they thought.

→ Getting pricing right the first time matters. Going back to renegotiate later adds months and real risk.

She had 11 years of commercial pricing instincts. Building her GSA pricing strategy was a completely different conversation.

The good news: once it's done right, it protects you.
