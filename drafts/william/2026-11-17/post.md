A construction contractor called me last month. He'd been in business for nine years, doing solid commercial work, and he was skeptical about the federal market.

"My clients are real estate developers and general contractors," he told me. "I don't see how the government fits in."

I asked him to look up his NAICS code on USAspending.gov. Just to see the number.

He called me back an hour later. Construction contractors — companies his size — received $4 billion in federal awards in Q3 2026 alone. Not the year. One quarter.

"I had no idea," was all he said.

That's the conversation I have more than any other. It's not that small businesses don't qualify. It's that most of them have never looked at the actual market size for their industry.

The opportunity is there — most owners just haven't looked up the number yet.
