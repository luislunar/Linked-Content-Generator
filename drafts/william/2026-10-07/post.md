"Do we even qualify if we've never won a federal contract?"

I get that question on almost every discovery call with small business owners who are new to the federal space. And I completely understand why — past performance feels like the entry fee.

Here's what I tell them: it matters for some set-asides, but it's not the wall you think it is. Teaming is how most small businesses cross that threshold.

What teaming actually means in practice:

→ A teaming agreement is a formal arrangement where your company partners with a prime that already has the past performance you don't yet have.

→ You bring the specialized capability — skills, pricing, capacity. They bring the federal credential. The agency sees a combined team, not a gap.

→ You don't have to wait until your GSA Schedule is approved. You can sub on contracts while your own application is still in progress.

→ Finding primes isn't guesswork — sources sought notices, agency industry days, and SAM.gov prime award data show exactly who's winning in your space and actively looking for subs.

→ The work you do as a sub becomes your past performance for the next opportunity. It compounds.

The small businesses I see break in fastest are almost never the ones waiting until everything is perfect. They start building the record while they build the credential.
