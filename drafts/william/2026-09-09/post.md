Before you start your GSA Schedule application — can you actually answer these five questions?

Most owners I talk to say yes. Then we go through them together, and usually we find at least one gap.

Here's what I walk through on every first call:

→ Two years in business
Federal eligibility has a hard floor. No waivers, no workarounds — you need the track record.

→ Tax returns filed — both years
GSA reviews your financials as part of the application. If they're not filed or there are gaps, the process stalls before it ever gets moving.

→ Active SAM.gov registration
Contracting officers check SAM before they call anyone. If your registration is expired or incomplete, you're invisible — and you won't know it.

→ A commercial price list you can defend
Your GSA pricing is built off what you charge real commercial clients. "Around $X per hour" is not a price list. You need something documented.

→ Evidence of commercial delivery
Not federal work, not million-dollar contracts. Just proof — projects, invoices, client letters — that shows you've done the thing you're selling.

Most people I talk to have four of the five. That missing piece is almost always fixable — but you want to find it before the application does.
