This draft tests whether a cautionary compliance story — framed as a question that challenges an assumption Schedule holders carry right now — drives higher comment engagement than informational or myth-bust formats. The question hook ("when did you last log in?") is designed to surface latent anxiety in two distinct cohorts: owners who actively hold a Schedule and may not know its current status, and prospects evaluating whether the ongoing maintenance burden is real. The before/after template makes the stakes concrete and scannable, while the narrative arc (call observation → mechanics → emotional consequence → closing principle) gives the post a structure that earns the CTA rather than appending it. Expected score movements: reactions from holders who recognize the scenario and prospects who bookmark it; comments from holders sharing their actual compliance status or asking what thresholds to watch; possible inbound from owners who discover mid-scroll that they haven't checked their Schedule in months.
