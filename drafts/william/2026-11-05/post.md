Before you assume your GSA Schedule is still active — when did you last log in?

I ask because of a call I had last month.

The owner had been approved three years ago. Construction services. He'd been meaning to start pursuing federal work "once things slowed down."

By the time things slowed down, the Schedule had been cancelled.

Not because of anything scandalous. His SAM registration had lapsed. A modification window closed unanswered. Without active reporting, the Schedule went dormant — and then GSA closed it.

What I walk through with owners after something like this:

→ Approval isn't permanent. GSA Schedules require active maintenance — renewed SAM registration, timely modification responses, meeting minimum reporting thresholds.
→ Dormancy has a deadline. You can let a Schedule sit quiet for a while, but there's a window before it closes permanently.
→ Reapplication isn't a shortcut. Coming back in means restarting the 6–12 month process, not picking up where you left off.

The hardest part of that conversation wasn't the news. It was knowing he'd had 36 months of access sitting there while the clock ran out.

Staying compliant isn't just about getting approved. It's about staying approved long enough to actually use it.
