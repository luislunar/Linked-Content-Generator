Before you talk yourself out of applying for a GSA Schedule — these are the five things I hear most on first calls.

Almost every one of them is the exact reason someone hasn't started yet.

**1. "My company is too small."**
The Schedule was built for small businesses. Most of the owners we talk to were doing under $5M in commercial revenue when they applied.

**2. "I need past performance before I can get on."**
Not for the Schedule application itself. Past performance matters more once you're pursuing specific contracts — it's not an upfront requirement.

**3. "The process is too expensive."**
There's no government application fee. The real cost is time: 100+ hours of paperwork, compliance, and negotiation. Done-for-you services exist specifically to solve that.

**4. "Once I'm approved, contracts come automatically."**
The Schedule is an access credential, not a revenue guarantee. Outreach, positioning, and follow-up are what actually generate results.

**5. "My industry isn't a good fit."**
Engineering, construction, IT, professional services — small businesses in these sectors captured billions through federal awards in Q2 2026 alone.

I'm not here to convince everyone to apply. But most of the owners who haven't started are stuck behind something that isn't true.

Which one of these held you back — or almost did?
