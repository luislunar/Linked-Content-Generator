A prospect called me last month.

Two years on SAM.gov. Zero federal revenue.

He'd been watching his NAICS codes on USASpending. Every week. Waiting.

"I don't understand why nothing's moving," he said.

I asked when he last responded to a Sources Sought.

He had to think about it.

That's the gap I see on almost every call. Owners who are on the list vs. owners who are working it.

The ones who move treat pipeline like a job, not a credential.

Here's what they do differently:

→ Respond to Sources Sought notices — even when they don't expect to win. Gets your name in front of the CO.
→ Attend industry days to learn who's already on the vehicle, not to pitch.
→ Set SAM.gov alerts by NAICS code, not by agency — opportunities come to you.
→ Call contracting offices. Not to sell. To ask what's on deck for the next fiscal year.

The Schedule gets you in the door.

Working the pipeline is still on you.
