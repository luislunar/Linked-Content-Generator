A question I hear on almost every first call: "Do we actually qualify for this?"

And then — usually five minutes in: "How long does it take?"

I answer the same questions so often I started writing them down. Here's what comes up on almost every call about the GSA Schedule:

→ **Do we qualify?** Two years in business, commercial revenue, clean SAM.gov registration. That's the short answer.

→ **How long will it take?** 6–12 months is typical. Organized financials and solid past performance docs make a real difference.

→ **Will we actually get contracts?** The Schedule is market access — not a contract guarantee. You still have to sell.

→ **Can we do it ourselves?** Yes. Most people who try come back for help around page 40 of the solicitation.

→ **What if we get rejected?** Our team holds a 98% approval rate and offers a money-back guarantee if we don't get you there.

These aren't hard questions — just ones nobody answered before the first call.
