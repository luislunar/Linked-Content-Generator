This draft tests whether a data-anchored call story — using a real industry figure to anchor a pipeline insight — outperforms pure caller-pattern posts for the behind_scenes post type. The $4.0B construction figure creates a concrete "reveal moment" that readers in construction (and adjacent sectors) can personally anchor to, lowering the abstraction barrier of a typical pipeline post. Expected score movements: reactions (owners who had the same realization, plus construction/contracting professionals validating the number) and comments (the specific CTA asks for a named tactic rather than a general experience, reducing friction for respondents who have a concrete answer). The two-direction visibility framing ("you can't see the pipeline AND the pipeline can't see you") is intended to generate a second wave of comments from owners who recognize the outbound problem but haven't thought about the inbound one.
