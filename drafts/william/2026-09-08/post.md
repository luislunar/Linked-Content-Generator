Nobody talks about what actually stops most small businesses from pursuing federal contracts.

Not the paperwork. Not the timeline.

A belief.

I take first calls every week with owners who have solid revenue, a proven team, and real capability in their field. By almost every measure, they're ready.

And the most common reason they haven't moved forward?

"We don't have past performance in federal. We don't qualify."

Here's what I actually hear when I push past that statement:

→ They've done the same work — just for commercial clients, state agencies, or large primes
→ They've been in business two-plus years with consistent revenue
→ They're in industries with strong federal demand: IT, professional services, construction

They're not unqualified. They've just never seen a clear picture of how federal contracting actually works at their stage.

The past performance requirement isn't the wall it looks like from the outside. For a GSA Schedule, it's far less about prior *federal* contracts and more about documented proof that you've delivered — anywhere.

That's a very different bar than most people assume.

If "we're not ready yet" has been your answer, what specifically made you feel that way? I'm genuinely curious where that belief started.
