Nobody warns you what happens to your GSA pricing when you skip the commercial rate audit.

I talk to business owners every day. The ones who negotiated their own Schedule rates often locked in prices that make them uncompetitive on every RFQ that lands.

Here's what I hear most:

→ Quoting the rack rate instead of your actual best commercial price — the CO will find the gap
→ Missing the Basis of Award structure — if your commercial price drops later, your GSA price must too
→ No escalation clause — inflation quietly erodes your margin over a 5-year base period
→ Underpricing a category to "win" — you're locked in at that rate until you file a formal modification
→ Skipping price reasonableness — a rate that feels fair to you may not survive GSA review

Pricing isn't the part people dread. It's the part that determines whether the Schedule ever makes money.
