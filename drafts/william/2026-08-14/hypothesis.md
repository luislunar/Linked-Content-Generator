This draft tests whether a question-hook that opens with a verbatim first-call objection — "Don't we need past performance?" — activates recognition faster than a declarative hook. By naming four specific myths rather than one, the closing CTA ("which one almost stopped you?") gives readers four concrete referents to respond to, lowering the friction for commenting. Expected score movers: comments (specificity of the anchor list makes self-selection easy) and reactions (the ICP tends to react strongly when a belief they held is named and gently corrected). The `behind_scenes` framing — "I hear this every week" — reinforces William's call-based credibility without any founder-level claims.
