A question I get on almost every first call: "Don't we need past performance before we can apply for a GSA Schedule?"

No. But it's one of four myths I run into every week.

The ones that come up most:

→ "You need federal past performance." — Your commercial track record is what matters most. Prior federal contracts aren't required to get on Schedule.

→ "The Schedule is just for IT or consulting." — Construction, healthcare, products, training — MAS is broader than most people picture.

→ "Once you're approved, agencies find you." — The Schedule creates eligibility, not visibility. You still need to reach contracting officers and respond to RFQs actively.

→ "The application takes years." — Typical timeline is 6–12 months. Most of the delay comes from the prep work, not from waiting on GSA.

Every one of these has kept a qualified business from taking a step they were ready to take.

Which one almost stopped you — or is still holding you back?
