Nobody warns you on the approval call.

You get the email. Schedule approved. Congratulations. And then it's quiet — until it isn't.

I hear some version of this on calls every few months: "I didn't know I had to report sales quarterly." Or: "Wait — if I lower my commercial rate, I have to notify GSA too?"

The GSA Schedule is a live contract. It doesn't sit in a drawer.

From the day you're approved, the obligations run alongside you:

→ Quarterly IFF reporting (0.75% of Schedule sales, submitted in the Vendor Portal)
→ Price Reduction Clause — a commercial discount can trigger a required GSA price match
→ Modification deadlines when your scope, labor categories, or pricing changes

None of this is complicated once you know it. The problem is that nobody hands you the checklist at approval.

The Schedule is where the work begins.
