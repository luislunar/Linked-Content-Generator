This draft tests whether a short truth-format compliance post (under 160 words) outperforms William's recent mid-length posts on reaction rate. The opener ("nobody warns you on the approval call") is designed to mirror the lived experience of Schedule holders who received no compliance briefing at approval — expected to trigger immediate self-identification reactions. The three embedded obligations (IFF reporting, Price Reduction Clause, modification deadlines) give the post concrete utility without turning it into a checklist, preserving the warm observational tone. Score components expected to move: reactions (self-identification from current holders) and comments (the CTA asks for a specific memory — first compliance surprise — which is a low-barrier, high-specificity prompt that tends to draw named replies rather than generic ones). The shorter length relative to William's last three posts tests whether reduced word count improves scroll-stop rate on a purely prose-led format.
