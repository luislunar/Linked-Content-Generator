This draft tests whether a story-hook tips_list — opening with a concrete field contrast ("same Schedule, zero results vs. three quotes") — drives stronger engagement than the recent checklist/question format. The "two identical owners, different outcomes" structure creates narrative tension that should increase scroll-pause and reactions, because it reframes a common frustration (Schedule approved, no awards) as a solvable process gap rather than bad luck. The four-habit numbered format gives holders something to validate and prospects something to immediately act on. Expected score components to move: reactions (recognition from holders who built this routine) and comments (prospects naming which habit they're missing, holders sharing what actually worked for them). The CTA asks about *current* pipeline routines, which is concrete enough to surface both social proof from active holders and urgency from owners who haven't built the habit yet.
