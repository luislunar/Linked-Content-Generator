I talked to two IT business owners last month.

Same GSA Schedule. Same NAICS codes. Roughly the same company size.

One had three active federal quotes in progress. The other had zero opportunities after eight months.

Same approved Schedule. Completely different results. The only difference was what each one did after approval.

Here's the pipeline routine I see in the owners who actually land federal work:

→ Set up saved SAM.gov searches for Sources Sought notices in their NAICS — checked every week, not every quarter
→ Tracked recompete cycles on contracts their target agencies already had running — 90 days out is when the prep starts
→ Responded to every industry day in their lane, even when they didn't have a Schedule yet
→ Built a short contact list inside two or three target agencies before any RFP ever dropped

The Schedule is the key. But this routine is what gets you to the door before it opens.
