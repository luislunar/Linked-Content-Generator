Before you respond to your next sources-sought notice, let me ask a quick question: when did you last actually log into SAM.gov and verify your registration is still active?

Not assumed. Not remembered from when you first registered. Actually logged in.

This comes up more than I'd expect on calls. A business owner is ready to respond to an RFQ — sometimes mid-application with us — and we discover their SAM registration expired months ago. No one flagged it. They had no idea.

Three things worth checking today:

→ Expiration date — SAM renewals are annual. Log in and confirm yours isn't within 30 days of lapsing.
→ Points of contact — outdated POC info can stall everything from renewals to award notifications.
→ NAICS codes — a code added at registration but never updated can quietly misrepresent what your company actually does.

The renewal itself takes minutes. Missing the deadline can pause your entire federal pipeline.
