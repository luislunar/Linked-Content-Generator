A question I hear on nearly every discovery call:

"How do I find opportunities before my competitors do?"

Here's what I keep coming back to: **Sources Sought notices.**

Before a formal RFP ever drops, agencies test the market. They want to know who qualifies, what the landscape looks like, what pricing might look like. It's not procurement yet — it's market research.

If you're responding to those notices, you're in the room before the requirements are even written.

If you're waiting for the final solicitation to show up on SAM.gov, you're already one step behind.

That gap between Sources Sought and award? That's where the real pipeline gets built.
