A question I hear almost every week — usually from someone with a fresh SDVOSB or WOSB certificate in hand:

"We just got certified. So... how do we start winning federal contracts?"

It's a reasonable question. The honest answer usually surprises them.

A certification opens the door to set-aside solicitations — opportunities reserved for businesses like theirs. That's real, and it matters. But the cert by itself doesn't generate pipeline.

Here's what I walk through on that call:

→ **The cert qualifies you. It doesn't market you.** Contracting officers don't search for certified businesses and hand out work. You still have to show up where the opportunities are.

→ **You need a contract vehicle.** Most federal set-aside work flows through GSA Schedule or agency-specific vehicles. The cert without a vehicle limits where you can actually bid.

→ **A capability statement is non-negotiable.** When a CO or prime does reach out, you have about 60 seconds to explain your value. If that document isn't ready, you lose the moment.

→ **Teaming accelerates the cert's value.** Pairing an SDVOSB or 8(a) status with a prime already on a vehicle is often the fastest path to a first award — especially without past performance yet.

The owners who make the most of their certifications treat them as a multiplier on a strategy they're already building — not as the strategy itself.
