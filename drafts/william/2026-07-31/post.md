Nobody talks about the moment a business owner realizes federal pipeline isn't like their commercial pipeline.

I had a call last week with a construction company owner. He'd been building commercial projects for 12 years. Smart guy. He knew his market cold.

But when I asked how he planned to find federal work after getting his Schedule, he went quiet.

"I figured I'd just... show up on SAM.gov and apply."

That's where we spent the next 20 minutes.

Federal pipeline is built before you win a contract — not after. It lives in sources sought notices, industry days, agency forecasts, and recompetes. Most of that lead-time work starts 6–18 months before a solicitation even drops.

What changed for him after that call: he stopped seeing the Schedule as the finish line. It became the starting block.

That shift in thinking is the one I see make the biggest difference — once the hard work of getting approved is behind you.

What's something nobody told you early about federal pipeline? I'd love to hear it.
