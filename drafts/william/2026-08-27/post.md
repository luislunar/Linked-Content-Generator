Nobody warns you that pricing too low on your GSA Schedule is a bigger threat than pricing too high.

I hear the same fear on almost every pricing call: "What if the government thinks our rates are too high?"

The fear is backward.

GSA negotiators will push back on your rates — that's part of the process. Your job is to anchor high enough that you have room to move. The companies that under-price to win approval fast end up stuck. Those rates lock in. Every quote, every task order, every recompete runs off the same numbers — sometimes for years.

Want to renegotiate? That's a formal modification, not a quick call.

What most business owners don't realize is that the pricing conversation is the most important one to have before a single page gets submitted. Not after you're approved and the margins already don't work.
