What does your federal pipeline actually look like right now?

Not your GSA Schedule status. Not your SAM registration. Your actual pipeline — the sources sought you're monitoring, the contracting officers who know your company name, the RFQs you're actively tracking.

I had a call last month with an IT services owner who had done everything right. Schedule was approved, SAM was current, certifications in order. Then he waited.

Eight months. Almost no activity.

He reached out wondering if something was wrong with his registration. Nothing was wrong with his registration. He just hadn't built any pipeline activity.

When we dug in:
→ Sources sought notices in his NAICS codes were going unread — he'd never set up alerts
→ No capability statement had gone out to the agencies he'd been serving commercially
→ He hadn't attended a single industry day or submitted a market research response

The Schedule is a door that opens. It doesn't walk through on its own.

Three months after working on the active side of it:
→ He's checking sources sought every week
→ Two contracting officers now recognize his company name
→ His first formal RFQ response went out last week

The change wasn't complicated. It was just intentional.
