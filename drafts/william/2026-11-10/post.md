A question I hear on almost every first call: "My SAM registration is active — we're good, right?"

Usually, the answer is: not quite.

SAM.gov is the foundation for everything in federal contracting. But most owners check it once at registration — and then assume it runs itself.

Here are five things that quietly break a SAM record between calls:

→ **Annual renewal slips** — SAM expires every 365 days. Miss the window and your contract payments can freeze. No warning email, just a lapsed record.

→ **NAICS codes too narrow** — if your codes don't reflect all the work you actually do, you're invisible to contracting officers searching by industry.

→ **Reps & certs not refreshed** — your representations and certifications have to stay current. Old data can disqualify a bid before the CO reads another line.

→ **Outdated bank info** — if you switched business accounts after registering, federal payments could land somewhere unexpected.

→ **A forgotten exclusion flag** — expired compliance filings or unresolved issues can trigger a flag you won't see until a CO runs your check.

The fix for every one of these is a 15-minute audit before you pursue anything.
