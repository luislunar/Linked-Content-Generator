Before you assume SAM registration makes you GSA-ready — here's what I actually hear on first calls.

Owners who've been in SAM for two or three years call and wonder why no contracts have come through. It happens more often than you'd expect.

Most of the time, the answer is the same.

SAM puts you on the map. A GSA Schedule puts you in front of buyers. Being registered in SAM means agencies can verify your business and send you payments — but it doesn't put you on a purchasing vehicle that agencies use to actually buy. Those are two different things.

The government doesn't explain this clearly, which is why the confusion comes up on almost every discovery call I have.

Once you see the difference, the next step gets a lot more obvious.
