Before you apply for a small business certification — do you know what it actually does on the federal side?

I ask this on almost every discovery call with a business that's exploring 8(a), WOSB, SDVOSB, or HUBZone. The answers I hear tell me a lot.

Here's what most people get right — and what they usually don't:

→ Certifications open set-aside opportunities. They don't guarantee you'll win them. You still need a competitive proposal.

→ Each has its own eligibility clock. 8(a) has a nine-year program life. WOSB has ownership and income thresholds. Audit the fine print before you start the application.

→ You can stack them. A WOSB that also qualifies for HUBZone can compete in both pools — a smaller, less crowded field than the open market.

→ Certifications amplify a GSA Schedule, they don't replace it. Set-asides still run through contract vehicles. Having both is the combination I see win most consistently.

→ Agencies verify. Annual recertification isn't optional — and falling out of compliance mid-award is worse than never having the cert at all.

The certification is the key. You still have to knock on the right doors.
