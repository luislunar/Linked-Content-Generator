Nobody talks enough about how small businesses negotiate against themselves — before the government ever says a word.

I hear it on almost every pricing call.

An owner walks in with solid commercial rates. Then second-guesses everything: "But maybe I should go lower — I don't want to seem too expensive to the government."

The government didn't ask for a discount. The owner volunteered one.

Here's what that move actually costs:
→ Your Basis of Award locks in that lower figure. It becomes your ceiling.
→ Future price modifications have to justify any upward change.
→ Competitors who held their real commercial price now look better positioned to the CO.

GSA pricing isn't a race to the bottom. It's a structured negotiation — and the businesses that understand that come out with margins they can actually build a federal practice around.

The Schedule is a purchasing vehicle — what you price into it is what you have to work with for years.
