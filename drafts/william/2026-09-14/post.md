Before you assume the agency is ignoring you — when did you last check your SAM.gov registration?

I ask because of a call I had last month with a small business owner who had been following up on three capability statements. Six weeks of outreach. Zero responses.

On our call, I asked him to pull up SAM.gov.

His registration had lapsed 47 days earlier.

Every email he sent after that expiration landed with contracting officers who couldn't award to an inactive vendor. Nobody was going to call and explain why the silence started. He had no idea.

Here's what I walk almost every prospect through on their first call:

→ SAM.gov registration expires every 365 days — it is not a one-time task.
→ The renewal reminder email exists, but it's easy to miss, filter, or land in spam.
→ A lapsed registration doesn't just close new doors. It can freeze payments on work already in progress.
→ Once you renew, active status can take 2–5 business days to update across the system.

He renewed the same afternoon we talked.

Two of those three contracting officers responded within the week.

The pipeline was never the problem.
