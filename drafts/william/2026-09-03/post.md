A company owner called me last week convinced she couldn't compete for federal contracts yet.

"We don't have past performance," she said. "That's the whole problem."

Here's what I told her — and what I tell almost everyone who calls with the same concern:

Past performance is your eventual goal. Not today's requirement.

Today, teaming solves it.

→ Find a prime already on the GSA Schedule in your space
→ Sub to them on a few contracts — that's how past performance actually gets built
→ Teaming agreements can be scoped around your specific capabilities and expertise
→ After 2–3 awards as a sub, you'll have the record to pursue primes on your own

She didn't know this was an option. Most first-time callers don't.

The path into federal contracting isn't always through the front door. Sometimes it's through a partner's door first.
