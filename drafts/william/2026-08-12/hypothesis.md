This draft tests whether validating the prospect's existing pipeline habits — rather than correcting a mistake — produces stronger emotional resonance and comment engagement than the more common "you're doing it wrong" frame. The "warming up the market for everybody else" quote is the emotional anchor: prospects who've been diligently building federal relationships without a contract vehicle will recognize themselves in it immediately. Expected score components to move: reactions (the "so close, but blocked" scenario is highly relatable for pipeline-active prospects researching the Schedule) and comments (the CTA asks for a specific missed opportunity rather than a general opinion, which lowers the friction to share a concrete, personal answer and surfaces high-signal leads who are actively encountering federal solicitations they can't respond to).
