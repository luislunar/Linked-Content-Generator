I pulled up USAspending.gov with her on the phone. GSA alone awarded $3.0 billion to small businesses in Q3 2026. Not to primes. To companies that look a lot like hers.

The conversation changed after that.

The Schedule doesn't hand you contracts. But it does put you in the room where federal buying happens — and most small business owners don't know that room exists until someone shows them.
