Before you chase your next federal opportunity — log into SAM.gov first.

Every day I talk to small business owners who are serious about federal work. They know their NAICS codes. Their capability statement is polished. But one thing almost never gets checked: their SAM.gov record.

Here's what I see on almost every first call: the registration has lapsed. Or the primary POC is someone who left the company two years ago. Or the listed NAICS codes no longer match what they actually deliver.

A Contracting Officer checks SAM.gov before they pick up the phone. If your record looks abandoned, they don't call.

This isn't a once-a-year task. SAM.gov registration expires automatically after 365 days. Your company information changes — your record needs to keep up.

Take 10 minutes this week. Log in. Review your record like it's the first thing a CO will see — because it is.

When's the last time you actually reviewed your SAM.gov profile — not just assumed it was fine?
