This draft tests whether a short truth-format inspiration post (~119 words) about SAM registration expiration drives engagement through recognition in the "approved-but-inattentive" cohort — business owners who have never verified their SAM expiration date because nothing has gone wrong yet. Opening with a verbatim caller question ("My Schedule is active, so I should be good — right?") is designed to trigger immediate self-identification and lower the comment threshold; readers who have said this exact thing on a call are primed to respond. Expected to move reactions (agreement from owners who know this gap exists in their own registration) and comments in two cohorts: owners who have been burned by a lapsed SAM and want to share the story, and owners who go check SAM after reading and want to report back. The community question presses a specific behavior gap ("when did you last actually look — not just assume it was fine?") rather than soliciting a general opinion, which should produce more concrete and varied responses than a broad GovCon question would.
