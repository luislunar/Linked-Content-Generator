Nobody warns you that SAM.gov doesn't send expiration reminders.

A question I hear on almost every discovery call:

"My Schedule is active, so I should be good — right?"

Not always.

When that happens, you're invisible to the agencies trying to pay you — no payment processing, no new awards, and nothing letting you know why.

The fix takes ten minutes once you catch it. The damage can take months to untangle.
