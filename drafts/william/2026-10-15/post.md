Have you ever talked yourself out of federal work because you didn't have past performance?

I hear this on almost every first call with a company that's been in business for two or three years. They're doing solid commercial work, they've got the capacity, and then they say: "We've never had a federal contract — I don't think we qualify."

Here's what I walk them through.

The GSA Schedule doesn't require federal past performance to get approved. It requires commercial past performance — and you probably already have that. So step one is usually: you qualify more than you think.

Step two is the harder conversation. Getting the Schedule is the credential. But winning your first contract without any federal track record? That's where teaming changes everything.

A teaming agreement lets you partner with a prime contractor who already has the past performance, the agency relationships, and the established position on a vehicle. You bring the capacity, the specialized skills, or the bandwidth. They bring the door-opener.

The companies I talk to who move fastest through their first year in federal work are the ones who spent the first 90 days mapping which primes were active in their space — before they even had their Schedule in hand.
