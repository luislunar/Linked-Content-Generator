A question I almost never hear at the start of a first call: "Should I get a certification?"

What I hear instead: "I already have my WOSB cert — so we're basically set up for government work, right?"

Had this exact conversation last week. The owner had done everything right. Two years in business, solid commercial revenue. Her certification was legitimate and hard-earned.

She just didn't know what it could — and couldn't — do.

Your certification opens a lane. It makes you eligible for set-aside contracts reserved for businesses like yours. That's real and that's valuable.

But eligibility isn't access.

Most set-aside opportunities require a contract vehicle to respond through. A GSA Schedule lets agencies work with pre-vetted vendors without a full open competition. Without one, the lane is open — but there's no car.

The owners who actually win set-asides hold both the certification and the vehicle.

If you have a certification right now — what contract vehicle are you using to respond?
