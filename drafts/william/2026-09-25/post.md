Nobody warns you that getting a GSA Schedule approved is the starting line — not the finish.

I hear this on almost every first call. Someone's been researching for months, knows the timeline, is ready to apply. When I ask what they plan to do once they're approved, there's usually a long pause.

"I'll get on the Schedule and start winning contracts" is the most common answer.

Here's what actually happens:

→ The Schedule is a credential, not a pipeline
→ It makes you eligible — it doesn't make you visible
→ Federal buyers still have to know you exist, trust your pricing, and have a requirement that matches

The businesses that get real traction early are the ones building agency relationships, responding to sources sought, and showing up at industry days before their approval even comes through.

The credential opens the door, but the work still has to happen.
