I had a call last week with an engineering firm owner who had been "looking for federal opportunities" for almost two years.

He'd checked SAM.gov a few times. Signed up for a few email lists. Sat through a couple of webinars.

When I mentioned that engineering services companies received $2.5 billion in federal awards last quarter alone, he went quiet. Then: "That's just last quarter?"

That's the gap I see on call after call.

The market isn't the problem. The approach is.

Here's what the prospects who actually build federal pipeline do differently:

→ They set up Sources Sought alerts before the solicitation drops — not after.
→ They know which agencies buy what they sell, not just "the government" in the abstract.
→ They track recompetes 18–24 months out, not 60 days out.
→ They show up at industry days as faces, not just names on a registration list.

None of this is hidden knowledge. But it takes consistency that most owners haven't built yet — because they're still waiting for the contract to justify the effort, instead of treating the effort as the path to the contract.

The pipeline comes before the award. Every time.
