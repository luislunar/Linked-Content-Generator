This draft tests whether a SAM.gov "before you bid" question-hook — grounded in a familiar first-call scenario and a three-item maintenance checklist — generates reply-depth by surfacing owners who haven't checked their registration recently. The CTA ("When did you last log in and check?") is a concrete, timestamp-grounded prompt rather than a blank opinion question; it should lower the barrier for both pre-schedule owners (who recognize the gap immediately) and current holders (who can share what surprised them on renewal), moving comments. The three-bullet format at under 140 words should improve read-completion rate, lifting reactions. SAM maintenance pain spans the full ICP — pre-schedule and current holders alike — so broad self-identification is expected to improve impression-to-reaction conversion across both cohorts.
