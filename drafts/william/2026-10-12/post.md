Before you bid on your next federal opportunity — when did you last log into SAM.gov?

I ask because almost every week I talk to a business owner who hasn't touched their registration in over a year. Some don't know it expires annually. Others changed their banking info... but forgot to update SAM.gov too.

A lapsed registration can disqualify a bid before anyone reads it. A NAICS code mismatch quietly removes you from set-aside competitions you should be in.

Three things I walk through on every first call:

→ Active status — renew inside the 60-day window, not after it lapses
→ NAICS codes — do they cover everything you actually deliver?
→ Remittance info — does it match your bank exactly?

SAM.gov is the infrastructure your federal pipeline runs on. It deserves more than a one-time setup.
