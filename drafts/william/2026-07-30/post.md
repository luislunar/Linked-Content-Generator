There's a call I keep replaying in my head.

A woman-owned construction firm. Six years in business, solid commercial revenue. She called us because she was waiting — four months in — for her WOSB certification to clear before starting her GSA Schedule application.

Her reasoning: "If I wait for the cert, my application will look stronger."

It's a completely reasonable assumption. And it cost her months.

Here's what I walked her through:

→ Your GSA Schedule doesn't require a certification to be approved — they're entirely separate processes
→ Certifications and the Schedule run on parallel tracks; neither is a prerequisite for the other
→ Every month you hold off on the Schedule is a month your pipeline isn't building

She submitted her Schedule application that same week.

Her Schedule was approved before her WOSB came through. When the certification landed, she already had an active vehicle and had been positioning for opportunities the whole time.

Certifications and the Schedule absolutely strengthen each other. But you don't have to run them in sequence.

If you're treating a cert approval as the starting gate for your Schedule application, that wait is probably the most expensive decision in your business right now.
