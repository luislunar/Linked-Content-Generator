I had a call a few weeks ago with an IT services owner who'd been watching VA procurement for months.

Strong company. Good team. Solid commercial revenue. The kind of business that would do well in federal.

But every opportunity she opened had the same line: *past performance required.*

She thought that was the wall. I asked her one question — had she looked seriously at teaming?

Here's what shifted the conversation:

VA awarded $3.6B to small businesses in Q2 2026 alone. (Source: USAspending.gov)

Most of those winners didn't start with federal experience. Many got their first award through a teaming arrangement — bringing real capability to a prime who already had the contract vehicle and the relationship.

Teaming isn't a workaround. It's the on-ramp.

→ You bring the capacity to do the actual work
→ The prime brings the schedule and past performance
→ You both deliver — and you walk away with a federal record the next solicitation will recognize

It takes work to find the right partner. It takes paperwork to formalize it. But it doesn't take years of waiting for a door that won't open.

That owner is in active conversations with two potential primes right now.

What's the real reason you haven't had the teaming conversation yet?
