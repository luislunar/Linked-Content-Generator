Before you ask if your company qualifies for the GSA Schedule — ask what you're actually trying to get.

Most of the calls I take start with some version of: "I've heard about the GSA Schedule. Is it worth it for a company like mine?"

That's actually the right question.

Here's what I tell people: the Schedule isn't a contract. It's a credential — the purchasing vehicle federal buyers need on file before they can work with you without launching a full competitive bid.

What it actually opens up:
→ Your rates pre-negotiated with the government
→ Visibility on GSA Advantage and eBuy, where agency buyers actively search
→ Access to RFQs that are simply closed to vendors without one

The door opens when you get the Schedule. You still have to knock.
