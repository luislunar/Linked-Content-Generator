"Is the GSA Schedule worth it for a business my size?"

It's one of the first questions on almost every call I take.

Because the honest answer depends on something nobody asks back: what do you want your business to look like in three years?

The Schedule isn't a revenue event. It's an access credential — it puts you on the list of vendors federal agencies can buy from. That's where it stops.

What happens after that is up to you.

The owners I see do well understood that going in. They didn't expect a contract in month one. They built relationships, tracked recompetes, showed up to industry days. By year two, things started moving.

The door is worth opening. But you have to know what you're walking into.
