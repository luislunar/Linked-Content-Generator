Nobody tells you this when you're starting out in GovCon:

The small businesses landing their first federal work aren't always winning their own prime contracts. A lot of them are subbing to primes who needed exactly what they offer.

I talk to owners every week who are waiting — waiting until they have past performance, waiting until they've won one contract, waiting until the timing feels right.

Then I talk to the ones who've already made it in. And almost every time, it started with a teaming relationship.

Here's what I see those businesses doing differently:

→ They identify primes who have active GSA vehicles in their NAICS
→ They send a targeted capability statement — not a generic one
→ They show up at industry days ready to pitch, not just listen
→ They get a teaming agreement in place before the RFP drops — not after
→ They follow up consistently, because primes are busy and memory is short

The hardest part isn't finding the opportunity. It's being prepared before the window opens.

If you're waiting until you're "ready" to reach out to primes — that's exactly the part worth rethinking.
