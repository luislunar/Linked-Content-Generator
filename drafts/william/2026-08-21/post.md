Nobody talks enough about what the GSA Schedule actually does for you.

On almost every first call, I hear some version of this question:

"Once we get our Schedule, the contracts will start coming in — right?"

I get it. The federal market is massive. The Schedule is the vehicle.

But here's the honest answer:

The Schedule makes you *eligible*.
Eligible is not the same as *winning*.

The businesses I see actually generating federal revenue treat approval as the starting line — not the finish line. There's a whole layer of marketing, proposal work, and relationship-building that follows.

What happens after approval is the conversation most people don't know to ask about.
