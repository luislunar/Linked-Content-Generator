A landscape contractor called me last week.

First thing she said: "I'm WOSB certified. How do I turn that into federal contracts?"

She'd put months into the certification. She was proud of it — rightfully so. But she assumed the certification was the destination. It's actually the beginning of a different conversation.

Here's what I told her:

Certifications like WOSB, 8(a), or SDVOSB narrow the pool of competition. They give you access to set-aside contracts that larger primes can't touch. That's real leverage.

But the agencies still need to find you. And the most common path agencies use is the GSA Schedule.

Certification + Schedule = the combination that actually moves the needle.

One without the other leaves a gap somewhere.

She'd done the hard part. The next step was filling in what was missing.
