Nobody on these calls talks enough about what happens after your GSA Schedule gets approved.

Getting approved is a real milestone — and it should be. But I talk to holders every week who treated approval as the finish line. Some of them are now in trouble and don't know it yet.

Here's what the application process doesn't prepare you for:

→ Quarterly sales reporting (72A / Industrial Funding Fee). Miss it and you're out of compliance.
→ Every product or service change needs a formal modification — not just an email to your CO.
→ Price updates can't sit frozen. GSA expects your Schedule to reflect your current commercial rates.
→ An idle Schedule isn't a safe Schedule. Low-activity holders get flagged. Some get cancelled.

The owners who get the most from their Schedule treat it like an active business relationship — not a certificate to file away.

The hardest call I have is the one where a holder finds out their Schedule is at risk because they assumed it would just stay active on its own.

That assumption costs more than it should.

If you have a GSA Schedule, when did you last log in and check its status?
