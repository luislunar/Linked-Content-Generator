This draft tests whether a behind_scenes call-story post about teaming — anchored to the specific insight that a prospect's first sub opportunity usually lives inside an existing commercial relationship — outperforms generic teaming advice on comments. The story format (no bullet list) and first-person call narrative are expected to increase reactions by creating an emotional "that's me" recognition moment. The CTA asks about assumptions dropped when entering federal work, a reflective prompt with lower friction than an open experience question, which should surface both lurkers who are still in the research phase and holders who remember the mindset shift. Expected gains: reactions (+) from the relatable story arc; comments (++) from the open assumption-drop prompt appealing to a wide audience stage.
