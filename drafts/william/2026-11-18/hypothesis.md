This draft tests whether a compliance/maintenance post using a question hook and before/after structure generates comments from two distinct cohorts simultaneously: approved-but-inactive Schedule holders who recognize the post-approval dormancy pattern, and pre-approval prospects who start thinking about maintenance planning now. The opening quote ("I got approved eight months ago...") is a near-verbatim replica of the most common follow-up call William takes — high recognition rate expected among the target cohort. The community question is intentionally double-sided, lowering friction for both groups and widening the comment pool beyond one experience tier. Expected score movers: comments (double-sided CTA targeting two named moments — post-approval stuck, pre-approval planning) and reactions (recognition-driven, both cohorts can relate). The before/after card reinforces the central insight visually without needing to read the post copy, increasing scroll-stop potential in the feed.
