The truth nobody mentions when your GSA Schedule gets approved:

The contract vehicle is the entry ticket. Building a pipeline is the actual game.

I talk to new holders all the time who got their Schedule and then waited. Months go by. No RFQs. No awards. They think something's broken.

Nothing's broken — there's just no pipeline yet.

Here's where to start building one:

→ **Set opportunity alerts on SAM.gov.** Filter by your NAICS codes. Relevant solicitations show up in your inbox instead of you hunting for them.

→ **Pull your category's award history on USAspending.gov.** Two years of data shows you which agencies actually spend money in your space — and how much.

→ **Respond to Sources Sought notices.** You probably won't win a contract at this stage. That's fine. You're building a name with Contracting Officers before the real opportunity posts.

→ **Flag recompetes expiring in the next 18 months.** These are the contracts most likely to go back out to bid. Early visibility puts you in the conversation before it's crowded.

→ **Book a vendor outreach session at your target agency.** GSA has these programs for Schedule holders. Most new vendors never take advantage of them.

Holding your Schedule is one milestone. Building a real pipeline is a different discipline entirely — and it starts the week you get that approval email.
