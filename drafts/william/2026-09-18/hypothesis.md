This draft tests whether a concrete teaming call-story — anchored in a clear before/after timeline (zero past performance → first federal invoice in eight months) — drives higher comment volume among owners who've hit the past-performance wall. The "first award doesn't have to be solo" closer repositions teaming from a Plan B workaround into a deliberate first-year strategy, which should lift reactions from prospects who've self-selected out of federal work on a false assumption. The community question is designed to surface two reply types: holders who went the teaming route (social proof comments that reinforce the strategy) and prospects actively weighing whether they need past performance before approaching GSA (discovery signal comments that show intent), both of which move the reactions and comments components of the engagement score.
