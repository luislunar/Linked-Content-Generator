I had a call a few weeks ago with the owner of a facilities maintenance company.

Good revenue, solid team, three years in business. By every measure, she was a strong candidate for a GSA Schedule. But when I started asking about federal contracting, she stopped me.

"I've looked into it. We don't have federal past performance. Without that, we don't have a shot."

I hear this on almost every call with a business that's new to federal work.

Here's the thing: past performance isn't the only way in.

Teaming agreements exist exactly for this. A company like hers partners with a larger prime — one with a GSA Schedule, existing agency relationships, and a proven federal track record. The prime wins the contract. She delivers the work as a subcontractor, and builds a verifiable record of federal performance in the process.

That record becomes her past performance for the next opportunity — this time, as a prime herself.

It's not a shortcut. It still requires finding the right prime, negotiating a teaming agreement, and doing the work well. But it's a real, open door. And most small businesses I talk to don't even know it exists.
