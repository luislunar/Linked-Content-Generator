Nobody talks enough about what teaming actually is for a company that has never won a federal contract.

Almost every first call, I hear the same thing: "We want federal work, but we have no past performance." And then they wait — as if the government is going to hand them a reference they had no chance to earn.

The catch-22 isn't real. There's a designed path out.

Teaming is a formal arrangement where your capabilities and someone else's federal track record become one proposal. Here's what each side brings:

→ You bring: technical capability, certifications (8(a), WOSB, HUBZone), competitive pricing, and capacity
→ The prime brings: Schedule access, CO relationships, approved past performance
→ The agreement covers: your defined scope, exclusivity on that bid, revenue split

The teaming agreement goes in writing before the proposal. That's not a detail — that's the whole structure.

Past performance is a clock. It starts ticking the day your first teaming award converts to your name.
