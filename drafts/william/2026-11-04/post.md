There's a question I hear on almost every first call — usually about twenty minutes in, once the prospect starts to feel like the Schedule might actually work for them.

"Once I'm approved, do contracts start coming in?"

It's a sincere question. And it tells me exactly where they are in understanding federal sales.

Here's what I tell them: the GSA Schedule is your access. It puts you on a pre-approved purchasing vehicle that federal agencies can buy from directly — without a full open competition every time. That's real value. But it doesn't bring buyers to your door.

A lot of owners I talk to have this picture in their head: get approved, appear on GSA Advantage, wait for the calls. What actually happens is different. The Schedule opens a door. Walking through it — consistently, over months — is on you. That means responding to Sources Sought notices, reaching out to Contracting Officers before RFQs drop, tracking the agencies that actually spend in your NAICS codes, and keeping your pricelist current and competitive.

The owners who tell me "my Schedule never did anything" usually treated approval as the finish line. The ones who made it work treated approval as day one of their federal sales effort.
