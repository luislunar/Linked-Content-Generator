This draft tests whether a short (≤180 word) story-format behind_scenes post, anchored to a single relatable discovery-call moment, outperforms recent checklist and inspiration formats on comment depth. The SAM expiration scenario targets two reply groups: businesses with active applications who will immediately go check their own SAM date (urgency creates reactions at the 10× multiplier) and experienced Schedule holders who've been burned by the same silent lapse and want to share the story (comment depth at the 30× multiplier). The "how long had it been sitting" question is designed to elicit a specific numeric answer, lowering the friction to reply compared to open-ended opinion prompts. Expected score components to move: reactions (self-recognition urgency from the "check today" close) and comments (story-sharing from holders with a SAM incident in their past).
