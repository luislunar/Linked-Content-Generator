I had a call last week with an IT services company.

Nine months into their GSA Schedule application. No award yet. They figured the government just moved slowly.

When we pulled their SAM.gov profile, the problem was immediately clear: their registration had lapsed at month six.

No alert. No automated hold. Just a silent clock that expired while they were waiting.

The government can't make awards to entities with expired SAM registrations. It's not a technicality they'll waive. The whole pipeline freezes — quietly.

We got the renewal done in a day. The application started moving within a few weeks.

→ SAM.gov renewal is annual — no exceptions.
→ There's no reliable system alert before it lapses.
→ A lapsed registration doesn't visibly fail. It just quietly freezes everything.

Check your expiration date today if you have an active application or a live Schedule.
