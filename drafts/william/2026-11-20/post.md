A question I get almost every week:

"We don't have any federal past performance. Are we locked out?"

No. But you do need to build your first reference somewhere — and teaming is usually how that happens.

What I walk callers through:

→ Past performance is team-level, not company-level
When you sub to a prime, the work you contribute counts as performance you can reference in future bids. That record follows you.

→ Your certifications are real leverage
8(a), WOSB, SDVOSB, HUBZone — primes need these to hit set-aside requirements on certain contracts. That's not charity. That's value you bring to the table.

→ Get the teaming agreement in writing
A handshake isn't enough. Scope, labor split, and your work percentage need to be documented before the RFP drops. No paper, no deal.

→ Sources Sought is where the relationship starts
Agencies post them to gauge the market. Primes read them too — they're actively looking for capable subs to bring into their bid. Responding early puts you in the room before competition heats up.

→ One solid sub performance opens more doors than a cold prime application
I've seen owners spend a year trying to win as a prime before realizing a 6-month sub role would have set up everything they needed.

You don't need a perfect record to get started. You need the right team.
