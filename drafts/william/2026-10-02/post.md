A question I hear on almost every discovery call: "We got the Schedule eight months ago. Why aren't we seeing any federal work?"

The answer isn't the Schedule.

It's the pipeline that feeds it.

I was talking with an IT services owner earlier this year. Same story — approved, credentialed, waiting. Six months in, zero federal revenue. She assumed RFQs would show up once she was listed on the contract vehicle.

Here's what changed when we walked through her pipeline together:

→ She started monitoring sources sought notices in her NAICS codes — not just waiting for the final solicitation to post.

→ She registered for two agency industry days she didn't know were open to small businesses.

→ She mapped out three recompetes coming due in the next 12 months and started tracking the incumbents.

Sixty days later, she'd submitted two capability statements, attended one industry day, and opened a first conversation with a Contracting Officer.

No award yet. But a real, active pipeline — one that simply didn't exist six months before.

The Schedule is the credential. The pipeline is the work.
