Nobody warns you that SAM.gov isn't a one-time setup — it's a living record.

I hear this more than almost anything on calls. Someone just earned their GSA Schedule approval — and we discover their SAM registration lapsed two months ago. Everything slows down at exactly the wrong moment.

Here's what I've seen trip people up:

→ SAM.gov renews annually — the reminder email goes to whoever registered (often someone who's since left)
→ NAICS codes don't update on their own — agencies search by NAICS, so an outdated list makes you invisible in searches
→ Point of Contact info stays frozen from initial registration — wrong name, old email, dead phone
→ "Active" in SAM isn't the same as "current" — representations and certifications have their own expiration dates
→ Banking details can be outdated too — stale EFT info means delayed or misdirected payments

One field out of date at the wrong moment can pause everything just as things are gaining traction.

When did you last log in and actually review your SAM record — not just confirm it's active?
