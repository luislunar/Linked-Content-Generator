When's the last time you actually logged into your SAM.gov account?

I ask that on almost every first call I take. The most common answer I get: "Uh... when I registered. Maybe a year ago."

That's the moment I know we have some work to do.

SAM.gov isn't a one-time registration — it's an active record. The first thing a Contracting Officer checks before an agency will pay you, consider your bid, or even respond to your capability statement. What your profile says right now is either opening doors or quietly closing them.

The problems I find most often are completely invisible to the owner: lapsed registrations, NAICS codes that haven't been updated since the company pivoted, a former employee still listed as the primary point of contact.

None of it is catastrophic. All of it is fixable. But a CO isn't going to call and tell you.

When did you last review yours?
