This draft tests whether a carousel myth-bust format with a question hook drives higher comment volume than the single-image posts used the past three days. The hook ("What if the biggest reasons you've avoided the Schedule aren't real?") is designed to create immediate personal identification — readers who have believed any of the three myths feel called out before they swipe. The three myths (past performance, company size, passive inbound) represent the highest-frequency objections encountered in first calls, which means a significant portion of the ICP audience holds at least one of them. The carousel format rewards engagement depth: each swipe is a micro-commitment that increases the probability of a comment response. The close slide CTA ("Which one almost stopped you?") is personal and low-friction — it invites a one-word or one-sentence drop rather than a long explanation. Expected to move comments (high — personal CTA + myth identification) and reactions (moderate — carousel slides tend to generate saves and reposts from people who want to share the myth corrections).
