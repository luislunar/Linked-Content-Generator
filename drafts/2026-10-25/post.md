You got your GSA Schedule approved. Now what?

Most holders treat approval like the finish line. It's not. The Schedule is your credential — the pipeline is built separately, and most people have no idea where to look.

Here are five places your next federal opportunity is actually hiding:

1. **GSA eBuy** — RFQs posted exclusively to Schedule holders before they go anywhere else. If you're not logging in weekly, you're missing solicitations your competitors are seeing.

2. **Sources Sought notices** — Pre-solicitation signals from contracting officers scouting the market. Respond within five days and you're already on their radar before the formal RFP exists.

3. **FPDS-NG recompete data** — Filter active contracts by expiration date. Contracts ending in 90–180 days are live competition windows. Most companies don't find them until it's too late to matter.

4. **Agency procurement forecasts** — Most civilian agencies publish annual acquisition plans. Find the agencies in your NAICS lanes, mark their award timelines on your calendar, and work backward.

5. **Industry days and market research calls** — Contracting officers need market research to justify awards. Showing up means you're a name they recognize when the solicitation finally drops.

The pipeline doesn't appear the day your Schedule is approved. It's built the year before.

Which of these five are you already working — and which one is furthest from your current routine?
