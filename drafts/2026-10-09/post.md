Chasing RFPs isn't a pipeline strategy. It's a reaction.

Most small businesses build their federal pipeline by waiting — SAM.gov posts a solicitation, they scramble to bid. That's not a pipeline. That's a fire drill.

The contractors who consistently win start months earlier. They respond to sources sought notices. They attend industry days. They reach out to agency program managers during market research windows — before a solicitation exists.

By the time the RFP drops, they've already shaped the requirement.

Sources sought isn't bureaucratic noise. It's your invitation to influence what gets written.

Have you ever responded to a sources sought notice? If so — did it open a door?
