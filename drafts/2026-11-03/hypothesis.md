This draft tests the `story` hook pattern on the `myths` topic using a `personal_story` format — a first-person field account that opens with a familiar prospect scenario before delivering the myth-bust. The past-performance objection is the single most common barrier Josh hears, which means readers will self-identify immediately: they've either said it themselves or coached someone through it. The narrative arc (belief → reality → path) mirrors a coaching conversation, making it feel like direct value rather than a post. The community question ("what belief almost kept you from exploring it?") invites experience-sharing rather than a yes/no, which should drive higher comment counts. Expected to move: **comments** (readers who've held this belief will want to share their version) and **reactions** (recognition/validation from those already in the market who've seen this pattern). The `single-07-story` cream template breaks the last two dark-navy cards visually, and the prose-only format (no bullets) contrasts cleanly with the previous `tips_list` and `hot_take` formats.
