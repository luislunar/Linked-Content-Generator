This draft tests a `blunt` hook pattern on the `pricing` topic using a `tips_list` format — a combination not used in the last 5 days. The opening line ("Your GSA pricing follows you for years") bypasses the typical myth-bust or alert opener and instead leads with a quiet, uncomfortable truth that should stop the scroll for any small business owner who has been vague about their GSA rates. The `single-06-checklist` card (dark navy) breaks the two-post streak of cream-background templates and gives the list visual weight.

The five items were chosen to span the full pricing lifecycle: negotiation input (Basis of Award anchor), ceiling-setting (rate too low), ongoing risk (Price Reductions Clause), upside capture (economic price adjustments), and margin protection (indirect costs). This breadth means readers at different stages of their Schedule journey will each find something personally relevant.

Expected score movers: **reactions** from GSA holders who recognize one or more of these mistakes from their own experience, and **comments** from readers sharing which specific error caught them — especially the Price Reductions Clause, which tends to be the least-known. The community question is an "experience-share" style that invites honest disclosure of a past blind spot, which typically drives higher comment volume than opinion-style CTAs.
