Before you decide federal contracting "isn't for companies like mine" — here's what Q3 2026 actually looked like.

Small businesses received $23.4 billion in federal awards across just eight industry categories in a single quarter. That's not a niche market. That's a parallel revenue stream running alongside your commercial clients — and most small business owners have no idea it's accessible to them.

The barrier isn't the market size. It's visibility. The pipeline is public, free, and updated constantly — USAspending.gov, SAM.gov Sources Sought notices, agency forecast reports. But you have to know the system exists before you can navigate it.

What most people miss: the companies winning those awards aren't bigger than you. They got on GSA Schedule, they read the forecasts, and they showed up when RFQs hit. That's the whole play.

Federal contracting isn't harder than commercial sales. It's different. And different is learnable.
