Before you copy-paste your commercial rates onto a federal RFQ, read this.

I had a call last month with an IT consulting firm that had been doing exactly that for two years. Good company. Real revenue. Solid team.

Their problem: the rates they were using on federal work were the same ones they quoted private clients. On the surface, fine. Under a GSA Schedule, that's a Basis of Award problem waiting to happen.

Here's what it looked like before they fixed it:

→ Commercial and federal pricing tied together — no separation
→ Quoted lower rates to commercial clients throughout the year
→ Contracting Officer issued a price reduction request under the BOA clause
→ Margin evaporated trying to stay compliant after the fact

After 90 days of cleanup:

→ Commercial and federal rate structures separated
→ Basis of Award clause fully documented and tracked
→ No more CO surprises — pricing stands on its own
→ Won a re-compete at a margin they could actually defend

The GSA Schedule doesn't require the lowest price. It requires a defensible one.

If you're running commercial and federal pricing off the same sheet today, you're one audit away from an uncomfortable conversation.
