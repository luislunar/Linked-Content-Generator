This draft tests whether a stat-led myth-bust in the `inspiration` format (short, ~120 words, no bullets) drives higher comment volume than the recent string of longer bullet-list posts. The "$22.4B in one quarter" figure is designed to kill the "federal contracting is for big companies" objection instantly — a line Jason hears weekly in prospecting. The open community CTA ("what myth almost stopped you?") invites first-person experience sharing, which typically generates higher-quality comments than yes/no questions. Expected score components to move: reactions (a hard, shareable stat = scroll-stopper) and comments (personal myth invites self-disclosure). The format shift away from bullets and the stat-as-hook also tests whether a quote-card template out-performs the checklist and bold-statement cards that have dominated recent posts.
