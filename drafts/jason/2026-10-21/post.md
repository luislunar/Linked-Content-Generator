"Federal contracting is for big companies."

I hear this one every week.

The numbers: small businesses captured $22.4 billion in federal awards in Q3 2026 — one quarter, eight industries, companies doing under $50M in annual revenue.

The market isn't the problem. The mechanism is.

You need the right vehicle to access it — a GSA Schedule, the right NAICS codes, or a certification that puts you on agency radars. That's the real barrier. Not the government's budget. Not demand. Not contract volume.

The money is there. The paperwork is what keeps most small businesses watching from the sidelines.
