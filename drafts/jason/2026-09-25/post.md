Contract vehicle health check: here's what most people miss.

Most small businesses I talk to assume the "best" contract vehicle is the one with the most agency coverage.

That's the wrong filter.

The right question: which vehicle can you get approved and start billing on in the next 12 months?

Here's how I work through it on calls:

→ Category first: SEWP V is IT-only — products and services. If you're professional services, engineering, or consulting, stop there. GSA MAS covers 12 large categories.
→ Timeline second: SEWP V procurement cycles run 18–24 months and only open periodically. GSA MAS typically approves in 6–12. If you need federal revenue before next fiscal year, you're not choosing SEWP V.
→ Certifications third: If you're 8(a) or WOSB, stacking your cert with a MAS Schedule unlocks set-aside opportunities that no other vehicle replicates at this stage.

I spoke with a company last quarter that had spent 14 months waiting on a SEWP V application — while a competitor got a MAS Schedule, found a contracting officer through SAM.gov, and closed their first federal award.

Not because SEWP V is a bad vehicle. Because it was the wrong vehicle for that stage.

The best contract vehicle is the one that gets you to revenue fastest given where you actually are today.
