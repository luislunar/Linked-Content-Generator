"SAM.gov registration is a one-time task."

That's the most expensive assumption I hear in sales calls.

SAM expires every 12 months. Miss the window and everything freezes:

→ Active awards go on payment hold
→ Open bids get auto-disqualified
→ Your contracting officer can't process new work

The worst part? GSA sends no reminder. The system just goes dark.

It takes 10 minutes to check your expiration date. It takes weeks to fix if you let it lapse.
