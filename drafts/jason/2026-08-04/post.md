"Should I get the GSA Schedule?"

Wrong question.

The real one: which contract vehicle are your target agencies actually buying from?

I hear the GSA-vs-everything debate every week. Here's the no-fluff breakdown:

→ **GSA MAS** — catalog buys, any civilian agency, broad categories. Open year-round. Lowest barrier. Best first vehicle for most small businesses.

→ **SEWP V** — IT products and services, NASA-managed. Competitive entry. Heavy DOD and civilian IT use — but it's IT only, don't force it if your offering is mixed.

→ **CIO-SP4** — NIH-managed IT services. Past performance required upfront. Higher bar than MAS, but once you're on, buyers come to you.

→ **OASIS+** — complex professional services at scale. Think $5M–$50M contracts. Hardest to get on, highest ceiling. Most small shops aren't ready for it on day one.

The shortcut nobody mentions: call your target contracting officer and ask what vehicle they've been buying from this fiscal year. That one conversation cuts through every whitepaper written on this topic.

The vehicle question matters less than the buyer question.
