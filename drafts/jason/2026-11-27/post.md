No federal past performance? Good — you don't need it to get a GSA Schedule.

The actual requirements to apply for GSA MAS:

→ 2+ years in business
→ $25,000+ in annual revenue
→ Relevant commercial work (federal history: optional)

That's it.

I hear "but we've never sold to the government before" at least twice a week. That objection isn't a barrier — it's the reason companies like yours apply for a Schedule in the first place.

The Schedule is the door into the federal market, not the prize for already being inside it.
