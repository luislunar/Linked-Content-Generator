The most expensive line in your GSA application isn't a fee.

It's the price you set.

I look at a lot of proposals. The pattern I keep seeing: companies submit their standard commercial rate, skip the Basis of Award negotiation, then wonder why they can't compete once the Schedule is live.

Here's what's actually happening. GSA locks your ceiling price at approval. Enter too high — or negotiate nothing — and you're boxed in forever. Competitors with better-negotiated rates undercut you on every single RFQ.

The fix isn't complicated:

→ Audit your commercial pricing *before* you apply
→ Identify your Most Favored Customer rate
→ Use that as your anchor, not your retail sheet

One pricing decision made in month one follows you for the life of that contract.
