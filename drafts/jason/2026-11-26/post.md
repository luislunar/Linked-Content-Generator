"The federal market isn't for small companies."

Wrong. That objection is precisely backwards.

Small businesses collected $23.4 billion across the top 8 federal contracting industries in Q3 2026 alone. One quarter — not the full fiscal year.

Computer services. Construction. Engineering. Consulting. Administrative management. If your company operates in any of these, that money was already moving toward businesses your size.

So what separates the ones who captured it from those watching from the sideline?

The paperwork.

→ No GSA Schedule → the CO searches SAM and you're not there
→ No SAM registration → invisible when the RFQ drops
→ No capability statement → primes move to whoever is ready

The government isn't loyal to Booz Allen. It awards to whoever cleared the eligibility hurdles first.

$23.4B went to small businesses that did the paperwork. That's the barrier — not the market.

What NAICS code does your business operate under — and do you know what federal agencies spent with small businesses in that code last quarter?
