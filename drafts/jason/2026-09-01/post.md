Which contract vehicle should you target first — GSA MAS, OASIS+, or SEWP V?

I get this question on nearly every discovery call. The answer is almost always GSA MAS. Here's the breakdown I actually walk people through:

**GSA MAS**
→ Broadest coverage — products, IT, and professional services under one umbrella
→ Works across virtually every federal agency
→ Fastest realistic path to first revenue once approved
→ Prerequisite for task orders on many other vehicles

**OASIS+**
→ Professional services only
→ Requires documented past performance at application — not "we've done similar work," actual federal contracts
→ Longer certification timeline than MAS
→ High ceiling, but a real barrier at the door

**SEWP V**
→ IT products and services only
→ Extremely competitive pool — large resellers already hold it
→ Check the existing pool capacity in your category before spending time on an application

The pattern I see most in early-stage conversations: companies skip MAS because OASIS+ sounds more premium. Then they hit the past performance wall and have to start over from scratch.

GSA Schedule isn't the consolation prize. It's usually the foundation the other vehicles sit on top of.
