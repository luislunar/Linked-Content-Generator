Before you pay someone to get you certified — do you know which agencies actually buy from that cert?

Most owners I talk to treat certifications like a golden ticket. Get certified, win contracts. That's not how it works.

Here's what our team actually sees across conversations:

→ **SDVOSB** is the fastest self-certification on SAM.gov. VA awarded $5.7B to small businesses last quarter — the biggest single agency. If VA or DoD isn't in your target list, the SDVOSB set-aside pool shrinks fast.

→ **8(a)** has the highest ceiling — sole-source awards up to $4.5M, no competition. But it's a 9-year SBA-supervised commitment. Worth it if your industry has active 8(a) set-asides. Not worth it as a box-check.

→ **HUBZone** is the most overlooked. If 35% of your workforce is in a qualifying zone, you may already be eligible. It opens set-asides most competitors can't even see.

→ **WOSB** is NAICS-specific. Powerful inside the right codes. Weak outside them.

The actual mistake: chasing certifications before locking in a GSA Schedule. The Schedule is your access to $750B in buying authority. Certifications determine the lane you run in. Wrong order means credentials with nowhere to land.

Map your target agencies first. Then pick the cert that serves them.
