"You just got your SDVOSB cert. Now the federal contracts will come."

That's what most people believe. Here's what actually happens:

Nothing changes on its own.

The objection I hear most on evaluation calls: "I've been certified for two years and haven't seen any federal activity." Almost always, the cert exists and the pipeline doesn't.

Here's why:

→ Certifications improve your odds on solicitations you actively pursue — they don't generate the solicitations
→ Most set-aside RFQs route through contract vehicles (GSA Schedule, SEWP, OASIS+) — no vehicle, no response allowed
→ Contracting officers searching for vendors use SAM profiles and Schedule catalogs, not certification databases alone
→ Without active prospecting, even a perfect cert stack sits idle

The math nobody shows you: a set-aside is a door your certification unlocks. But you still have to knock.

Cert + no vehicle + no prospecting = badge, not revenue.
Cert + vehicle + active pursuit = an actual channel.

The certification makes the CO's decision easier once you're in front of them. Getting in front of them is still on you.
