Before you say "I don't have past performance" — have you actually looked at teaming?

The objection I hear most from new prospects: "We can't win anything. We have zero federal past performance."

It's not wrong. It's just incomplete.

Here's what the team walks companies through every week:

→ Past performance isn't a solo game. Teaming agreements let you piggyback on a prime's history — legally, compliantly, on purpose.

→ The prime gets your capability. You get their credibility. The government gets a team. Everyone wins — if you structure it correctly.

→ The structure matters more than the relationship. I've seen great partnerships fall apart because the teaming agreement didn't define scope, workshare, or who owned the customer relationship post-award.

Three things to nail before you sign a teaming agreement:
→ Defined workshare — your percentage of work, in writing
→ IP and confidentiality terms — especially if you're sharing pricing
→ A path to prime — are you a permanent sub, or is there a growth plan?

Teaming isn't a shortcut. It's the ladder.

The question isn't "do I have past performance?" It's "who already has what I need — and what can I offer them?"

If you've done a teaming agreement, what's the one thing you wish you'd put in writing from day one?
