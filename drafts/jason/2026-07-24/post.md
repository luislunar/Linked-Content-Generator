"All set-aside certifications are created equal."

Not even close.

I talk to small business owners every week who are chasing the wrong cert for their situation. Here's how the actual ranking looks:

→ **SDVOSB** — Highest ROI if you qualify. VA and DoD set-asides run deep, and verification got cleaner after VA took over the process. Apply immediately if you're eligible.

→ **8(a)** — Nine years of prioritized access. The application is painful. No cert gives you more runway to build past performance while you're still learning how to compete.

→ **WOSB / EDWOSB** — Effective, but only in the right NAICS codes. Before you invest in this one, pull your target agencies' set-aside history and confirm they actually use it.

→ **HUBZone** — Real impact in specific regions and sectors. Oversold everywhere else. Run the research before you commit to the annual recertification grind.

The call I never want to take: "I got certified six months ago and I don't understand why nothing's happened."

Certification opens the door. It doesn't ring the doorbell.
