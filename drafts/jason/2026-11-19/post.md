"Which certification should I get?" Wrong question.

The right question: does the cert you're chasing match the agencies you're actually targeting?

Here's what I track across conversations with business owners working toward certifications:

→ 8(a) is the highest-leverage cert in the pool — but only if you use it. Nine years of eligibility, one contract, cert expired. That happens more than it should.

→ WOSB/EDWOSB opens set-aside doors. It doesn't walk through them for you. You still need an active pipeline.

→ SDVOSB gets VA attention — if VA agencies are in your NAICS codes. If they're not, the cert is decorative.

→ HUBZone is chronically underused. Offices with HUBZone set-aside budgets are actively looking. Most small businesses don't map their pipeline to those offices before they qualify.

The certification isn't the strategy. The strategy is picking the cert that fits your specific agency targets — then pursuing the right vehicles behind it.

I see businesses spend six months getting certified with zero pipeline plan behind it. The cert clears the door. You still have to walk through.
