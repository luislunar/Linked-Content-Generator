Having a socioeconomic certification is not a federal revenue strategy.

I talk to business owners every week who have their 8(a), SDVOSB, or WOSB cert and assume it's their ticket in. It's not.

Here's what I saw last quarter:

Company had SDVOSB for 3 years. Zero federal revenue. Zero.

They were pitching agencies directly, emailing contracting offices, attending every industry day they could find. Nothing was sticking.

→ The cert gave them preferred status — but preferred status in a procurement nobody could find them in.
→ They had no contract vehicle. No GSA Schedule. No way for a CO to actually buy from them.
→ Their NAICS codes weren't aligned to where the spend was going.

Once they fixed the vehicle and aligned the codes, the cert became a multiplier instead of a placeholder.

Three months after their Schedule was awarded: first set-aside RFQ. First response. First award conversation.

The cert didn't change. The infrastructure around it did.

The objection I hear most: "We have a cert, we don't need a Schedule."

The cert gets you preferred status. The Schedule gives the CO a legal way to buy from you. You need both.

If you've had your cert for over a year and haven't seen federal revenue, the cert is not the problem. The pipeline infrastructure is.
