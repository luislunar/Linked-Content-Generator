A certification doesn't win you contracts.

I know that sounds blunt — but I hear the opposite assumption on almost every prospecting call I take.

DoD paid small businesses **$19.9B** in Q2 2026. One agency, one quarter. That money doesn't care about your SDVOSB or HUBZone status unless you know how to position against the set-aside solicitations designed for it.

Here's what each major cert actually does:

→ **SDVOSB** — Opens DoD and VA set-aside lanes. Highest ROI for veteran-owned companies. Near worthless if you're not targeting those agencies.
→ **HUBZone** — Adds a 10% price evaluation preference in competitive bids. Meaningful when margins are razor-thin.
→ **8(a)** — Sole-source authority up to $4.5M (services). Nine-year runway. The fastest path to a first federal award for many new entrants.
→ **GSA Schedule + cert stack** — This is where it compounds. The Schedule is your vehicle; the cert gives you priority lanes on it.

A cert without a vehicle is a key with no door.
