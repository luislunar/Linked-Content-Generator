"Getting on GSA Schedule locks in my pricing forever."

That's the objection I hear most when companies hesitate on pricing. Here's what the numbers actually show.

Five pricing realities I see when reviewing proposals:

→ **Basis of Award follows you.** GSA prices against your best commercial rate, not a competitor's. That 30% corporate discount you gave last year? The CO will find it.

→ **Low doesn't win long-term.** Task orders reopen competition. If you undercut yourself to get on Schedule, you have no room left when it matters.

→ **No EPA clause = silent margin erosion.** Labor costs move. Without an Economic Price Adjustments clause, inflation eats your margins for years — with no mechanism to recover.

→ **Freight and ODCs need their own line items.** Bundling them into labor rates creates a pricing problem that shows up in every proposal you write.

→ **Pricing modifications take months.** Getting it wrong upfront isn't a quick fix. It's a 90-day process minimum.

Pricing is where most proposals get complicated before they ever reach an agency.
