Most small businesses ask for the GSA Schedule.

Fewer stop to ask: is this the right vehicle for us?

Here's the breakdown I walk through on every evaluation call.

**GSA MAS (Multiple Award Schedule)**
→ Open to most commercial products and services
→ Broad agency access — but you're competing against 20,000+ holders
→ Best fit if your offering already sells commercially and you want wide reach

**OASIS+**
→ Professional services only: management consulting, IT, engineering
→ More rigorous evaluation, stronger field of competitors
→ Best fit if you have solid past performance and want flexibility on task orders

**SEWP / CIO-SP4**
→ IT hardware, software, and solutions — tighter scope
→ Fewer holders in your exact lane means thinner competition
→ Best fit if your core business is IT and you can prove it

The companies that struggle aren't the ones who picked the wrong vehicle. They're the ones who never compared before committing.

One decision at the start saves 6–12 months of backtracking.
