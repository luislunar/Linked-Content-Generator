Wrong question: "Which certification should I get?"

Right question: "Does my certification pair with a vehicle agencies can actually use?"

I hear the first one constantly. The second — almost never.

The VA awarded $3.6B to small businesses last quarter alone. A significant chunk flows through SDVOSB set-asides. But here's what I see on calls: veterans holding the cert, no GSA Schedule, no active BD, no CO relationships.

The cert isn't the bottleneck. The strategy is.

Certifications are preference mechanisms, not revenue machines:
→ 8(a) unlocks sole-source authority up to $4.5M — if the CO knows you exist
→ WOSB and SDVOSB are powerful on GSA MAS — if your Schedule is active
→ HUBZone in a high-demand zone = real competitive edge — if you're actually bidding

What I watch move the needle:
→ Cert + active Schedule + consistent CO outreach = the trifecta
→ Cert alone, no vehicle, no follow-up = expensive paper

The companies winning on set-asides earned the certification and built the machine to use it. Not one or the other.

Which certification are you holding — and what's actually attached to it?
