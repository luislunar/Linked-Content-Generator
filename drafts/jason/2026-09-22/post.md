$26.2 billion went to small businesses from the Department of Defense alone — in Q2 2026.

One quarter. One agency.

I hear it on calls constantly: "The federal market feels locked up. Only the big primes win." I run the numbers every week. That's not what they say.

The pipeline isn't dry. The search strategy is broken.

Here's where to actually look:

→ SAM.gov — not just for registration. The contract opportunities tab is the primary public feed for federal RFQs and RFPs. Most people set it up and walk away.
→ GSA eBuy — if you hold a Schedule, agencies can post direct solicitations to your SIN. Most Schedule holders have never logged in.
→ Sources Sought notices — pre-solicitation market research. Responding puts you on a CO's radar before the formal RFP exists.
→ USAspending.gov recompete search — find contracts in your NAICS expiring in the next 12 months. Warm leads, no cold outreach needed.

The money is moving. It went to companies smaller than yours.

The question isn't whether the federal pipeline has room. It's whether you're positioned to see what's in it.
