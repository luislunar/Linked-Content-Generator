"Just use your commercial pricing as a starting point."

That's the assumption I hear most often when walking a new prospect through their GSA application. And it's the one that creates the most avoidable damage.

Four pricing assumptions I see on nearly every first application:

Assumption: Commercial rates are a safe baseline
Reality: The Price Reduction Clause ties your GSA price to every future commercial discount you offer. Your market rate becomes a trap, not a floor.

Assumption: Price low to get on schedule faster
Reality: Post-award mods to raise pricing are slow and frequently denied. You lock in whatever margin you agreed to at award — for years.

Assumption: Basis of Award details can wait
Reality: Undefined BOA = unpredictable price reduction triggers. This catches most clients 12–18 months after award, not at proposal.

Assumption: No economic price adjustment clause needed
Reality: Five-year contracts. No escalation built in. Your 2026 margins are your 2031 margins — minus inflation.

The government isn't trying to underpay you. These assumptions do it for them.
