Before you commit to OASIS+ or SEWP, answer three questions first.

I go through these on every vehicle conversation:

→ Do you have an active GSA Schedule?
OASIS+ and SEWP both require it. No Schedule means you're not eligible — period.

→ What does your past performance look like?
GSA MAS has the lowest barrier to entry. OASIS+ pools are evaluated against specific domains and thresholds. SEWP is IT-only. If you're under two federal awards, MAS is almost always the right starting point.

→ Which agency are you targeting?
Fifteen minutes on usaspending.gov tells you which vehicles that agency actually buys through. Most small businesses skip this step and spend months chasing the wrong door.

The pattern I see most: companies burning 12+ months on OASIS+ eligibility when they don't have the Schedule to build from in the first place.

The vehicle hierarchy is not complicated. It's sequential.

Start with the Schedule. Everything else stacks on top of it.
