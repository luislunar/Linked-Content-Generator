What happens after your GSA Schedule gets approved?

Most people I talk to have no real answer. They spent months on paperwork, cleared the hurdles, and then treated approval like a finish line. It's not.

Approval is the permission slip. The BD starts after.

Here's what I see inactive holders get wrong:

→ Your catalog is invisible if keywords and NAICS codes don't match how agencies search. Wrong codes = zero discoverability.

→ The Schedule doesn't sell itself. Agencies need to know you exist before they'll issue an RFQ. That requires outreach — not waiting.

→ GSA pricing drifts. If you haven't reviewed your rates since approval day, you may already be uncompetitive.

→ No BD cadence, no pipeline. Approval with no follow-up is just expensive shelf space.

The Schedule is a tool. You still have to pick it up.
