Contract vehicle health check: here's what most people skip before they pick one.

Most small businesses choose a vehicle based on name recognition, not fit. That's how you spend 6 months getting approved for something your target agencies don't actively use.

The four screens I run through with every prospect:

1. **NAICS fit** — does your primary code actually appear in the vehicle's scope? Not every code lives in every vehicle.
2. **Minimum order floor** — SEWP has a $10K minimum. If your average engagement runs $8K, that's the wrong rail.
3. **Active buyer agencies** — GSA MAS is broad. SEWP is DoD/NASA IT-focused. Pick the vehicle your buyers are already using, not the one you've heard of.
4. **On-ramp timeline** — GSA MAS: 6–12 months. SEWP: invitation-only, no open on-ramps. Know your cash flow window before you start the paperwork.

The vehicle decision isn't "which one sounds best." It's "which one matches where my buyers actually spend."
