The objection I hear most from small businesses who *should* already be on the GSA Schedule:

"We're not ready yet."

Here are the four reasons behind that — and why none of them hold up.

→ **"We don't have federal past performance."**
GSA Schedule eligibility doesn't require it. You need 2 years in business and commercial revenue. No federal contracts on your record? You're still in.

→ **"Federal contracting is for big companies."**
DoD alone pushed $19.9B to small businesses in Q2 2026 (Source: USAspending.gov). One quarter. The market is real — and your competitors are quietly entering it.

→ **"The application is too expensive."**
GSA charges zero upfront. The real cost is 100+ hours of documentation if you go it alone. That's a time problem, not a money problem.

→ **"Once we're approved, the contracts will come."**
The Schedule is a vehicle, not a sales team. You still do outreach, respond to RFQs, and build relationships with contracting officers. Approval is access. Access isn't revenue.

The "not ready" excuse usually collapses once you run the numbers.
