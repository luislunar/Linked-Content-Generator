Certification health check. Here's what each one actually unlocks — and what it doesn't.

The objection I hear most: "I got my 8(a). Why is my pipeline still empty?"

Because a cert is an access credential. Not a sales rep.

→ **SDVOSB / VOSB** — VA set-asides and beyond. The VA awarded $3.6B to small businesses last quarter alone. Being certified puts you in the running — not automatically on the solicitation.

→ **WOSB / EDWOSB** — set-aside competitions under specific NAICS codes. Agency participation varies more than most people expect.

→ **8(a)** — sole-source authority up to $4.5M (services) over 9 years. Requires SBA approval and consistent relationship-building.

→ **HUBZone** — 10% price evaluation preference plus set-asides. Value depends entirely on your zip code.

→ **None of these replace a GSA Schedule.** They're stackable advantages — not alternatives.

The cert opens the door. Your Schedule, your pricing, and your follow-up keep you in the room.
