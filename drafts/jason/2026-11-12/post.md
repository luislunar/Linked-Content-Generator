How many NAICS codes does your SAM.gov profile list?

Small businesses captured $23.4B in federal awards in Q3 2026 alone. That money is sorted by NAICS code before it goes anywhere. If your code isn't listed, that category of awards doesn't reach you.

The number I see most often when I pull up a prospect's SAM profile? Two. Sometimes one.

Here's what that actually costs you:

→ Contracting officers filter by NAICS before they filter by price, past performance, or location
→ Most businesses expand their services over time and never update SAM to match
→ An IT firm with four service lines but one NAICS code is invisible to 75% of the agencies looking for them
→ Adding codes is free, takes 20 minutes, and can open up dozens of new contract categories overnight
→ The objection I hear: "We added those when we registered." Usually when we audit it, there are gaps
