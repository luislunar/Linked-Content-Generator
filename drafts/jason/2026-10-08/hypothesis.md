This draft tests whether reframing the vehicle question as a "buyer-first" ordering problem drives higher engagement than a straight comparison list. The contrarian hook ("it's the wrong question") is designed to create a pause-and-read moment for owners who have already Googled vehicle comparisons without getting a useful answer. Expected score impact: reactions should climb because the aha-moment framing is more shareable than a neutral breakdown; comments should follow from the closing question, which forces the reader to reveal a concrete answer (their vehicle + whether it matches their pipeline) rather than a vague opinion.
