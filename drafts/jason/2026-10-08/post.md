"Which contract vehicle should I pursue?" is the question I hear most from new clients.

It's also the wrong question.

The right one: *Which agencies are you already talking to — and how do they buy?*

Here's the breakdown I give on every evaluation call:

→ **GSA MAS** — The default. 24 million+ line items, every agency, every category. If you don't know which vehicle yet, start here.
→ **SEWP V** — IT commodities only. NASA-led. Strong for hardware and cloud resellers. Not for services-heavy shops.
→ **OASIS+** — Complex professional services. High barrier to entry. Worth it if you're already doing federal revenue. Not a starting point.
→ **8(a) STARS III** — Excellent IT vehicle, but requires an 8(a) cert. If you have it, use it. If not, that's step one.

The companies I see waste time are chasing OASIS+ because it "sounds serious" before they have a single agency relationship in place.

Vehicle first, agency second = backwards.

Which vehicle are you on — and does your pipeline actually match it?
