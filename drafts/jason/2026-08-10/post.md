Before your next federal proposal goes in, check when you found out the opportunity existed.

Most small businesses discover an RFQ on SAM.gov and respond. That's late-stage pipeline. The scope's already locked. The incumbent's already had the CO's ear for months.

The businesses building real federal revenue show up before the solicitation:

→ Track Sources Sought notices
They surface 6–18 months before RFQs drop. Comment on them. Get your name in front of the CO before anyone's officially bidding.

→ Own 2 agencies deeply, not 20 lightly
VA, DoD, DHS, and USDA alone awarded $22B to small businesses in Q2 2026. Pick 2 that match your NAICS. Know their buyers. Know their cycles.

→ Map recompetes in FPDS-NG
Most federal contracts re-compete. Find the incumbent. Study their scope. Show up to industry days where the CO signals what comes next.

→ Register in agency portals before you need to bid
PIEE for DoD. Agency-specific systems for others. Some contracting officers only source from vendors already in their registry.

The businesses winning federal work aren't more capable — they're earlier.
