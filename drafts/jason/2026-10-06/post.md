Not to Leidos. Not to Booz Allen. Not to SAIC.

To companies with fewer than 500 employees — companies that look a lot like the ones I talk to every week.

"Only big primes win" isn't a market reality. It's a story you're telling yourself while competitors collect that revenue instead.

The data says you belong in this market. The question is whether you're ready to do the paperwork.

Which agency on that list operates in your industry? That's where I'd start the conversation.
