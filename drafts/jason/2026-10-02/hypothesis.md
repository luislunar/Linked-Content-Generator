This draft tests whether a short contrarian post grounded in a real prospect conversation outperforms longer educational formats on reactions and comments. The "on-ramp vs. highway" analogy is designed to be instantly shareable — readers who made the same OASIS+ mistake will tag peers or comment with their own story. Expected gains: reactions (relatable self-recognition) and comments (vehicle-targeting confessions from business owners still in this situation). The community question is intentionally two-part to lower the barrier: anyone can answer the first half even if they're embarrassed by the second.
