Here's what I hear multiple times a week: "Should we get on SEWP or stick with GSA MAS?"

Neither first. Your buyer first.

The vehicle doesn't determine your success. Your target agency does. Once you know the agency, the vehicle is usually obvious.

Here's how it actually shakes out:

→ **Broad civilian agencies (GSA, HHS, Agriculture, Interior)** — GSA MAS is your first move. Widest reach, most agencies can use it directly, lowest barrier to entry.

→ **Defense IT, technical services** — SEWP XI or CIO-SP4 are the right conversations. MAS has DoD access, but CO preference for dedicated IT vehicles is real and documented.

→ **8(a)-certified shop targeting DoD** — OASIS+ Small Business or 8(a) STARS III. That's where the competitive margin opens up if you have the certification to back it.

→ **VA-focused health and professional services** — VA FSS runs parallel to MAS. Most companies ignore it. Don't.

GSA MAS is not wrong. It's the most logical starting point for most companies — broad, accessible, relatively straightforward. But it's a starting point, not a destination.

Treating MAS as the only door is how small businesses leave agency-specific opportunities on the table for years without realizing it.

The vehicle is just the key. You still have to know which door to knock on.
