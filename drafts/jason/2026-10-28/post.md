"You need federal past performance before you can get on GSA Schedule."

I hear that one at least twice a week.

Here are the 4 myths I hear most from businesses that have been eligible for years — and didn't know it:

→ **You need federal past performance.** You don't. GSA Schedule evaluates commercial performance. What you've done for private clients counts.

→ **It costs too much to get started.** The barrier is paperwork, not cash. The investment is ~3 hours of your input. The administrative lift is the hard part — and that's what our team handles.

→ **Only big companies win federal contracts.** $23.1B went to small businesses in Q3 2026 alone. The market doesn't lack opportunity. It lacks properly positioned small businesses. (Source: USAspending.gov)

→ **The Schedule sells itself once you're on.** It doesn't. The Schedule is access — not a pipeline. No outreach, no wins.
