"We'll apply for the GSA Schedule once we have a federal opportunity lined up."

I hear this on calls more than any other reason to delay, every single month.

Here's the problem with that timeline: Schedule approval takes 6–12 months. Federal buyers can only issue purchase orders against active Schedule holders. So the sequence when you wait looks like this:

→ An RFQ hits your inbox
→ You don't have a Schedule
→ The buyer moves on to someone who does

The window doesn't hold open while you're still in procurement.

The companies winning federal contracts today didn't get their Schedule *because* an opportunity appeared. They had it on the shelf *before* one arrived — and that's what made them findable when it did.

The Schedule isn't a response to demand. It's what creates access to demand.
