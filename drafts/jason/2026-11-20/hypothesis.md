This draft tests whether a myth-bust framed as a verbatim objection → logical counter drives comments from prospects who recognize the delay trap in themselves. The opening quote is designed to trigger self-identification ("that's exactly what I said") before the logical counter lands. Expected score components to move: **comments** (people who've been in this holding pattern and want to share how long it cost them) and **reactions** (blunt, relatable insight from anyone who's deferred GovCon entry). The three-bullet sequence — RFQ arrives, no Schedule, buyer moves on — makes the cost of waiting visceral and sequential rather than abstract, which should increase save rates. The closing line ("The Schedule isn't a response to demand. It's what creates access to demand.") is the message reinforcement bet: a crisp reframe that works as a standalone share.
