"Small businesses can't compete on price in federal contracting."

That's the objection I hear most from IT and professional services companies right now. So I ran the numbers.

Small businesses collected $4.6 billion in computer-related services in Q2 2026. One quarter. Not the large primes — companies the size of the ones I talk to every week.

The pricing problem isn't that you're too expensive. It's that most companies structure their GSA price wrong from day one.

Three pricing errors I see consistently when reviewing proposals:

→ Quoting your flat commercial rate without adjusting for Basis of Award — that pricing ceiling will come back in CO negotiations and you won't have room to move.

→ Skipping labor category mapping — if agencies can't match your rates to a category, they skip you and go to whoever made it easy.

→ Misunderstanding the Industrial Funding Fee — it's built into your Schedule price by default. If you haven't accounted for it, you're already eroding margins before the first award.

The market has the money. The mistake is in the setup.
