Most small businesses approach teaming the wrong way.

They send 30 cold emails to primes: "Would you team with us?"

Wrong question.

The question a prime actually cares about: "What gap does this sub fill on my bid?"

I spoke with a company last month that had been chasing teaming partners for 8 months. Zero takers. No federal revenue. No past performance to show contracting officers.

We shifted the approach. Instead of broadcasting, they mapped two primes pursuing DoD IDIQ vehicles that needed commercial construction in a specific NAICS — one those primes couldn't self-perform.

They stopped pitching their company. They pitched the gap they fill.

Six months later:

→ Named sub on a $4.2M award
→ First past performance on the board
→ Three additional primes now approaching them

This is how teaming actually works. It's not a favor exchange — it's a value case you build for the prime.

Stop asking: "Who will take me on?"

Start asking: "Who needs what I build — and can I prove it to them?"

When you flip the question, the door opens.

What's your approach to teaming right now — are you pitching your company, or are you pitching the gap you fill?
