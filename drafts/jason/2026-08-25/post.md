Your GSA Schedule gets approved. You celebrate. Then you forget about it.

That's where most small businesses lose their edge — not in the application, but in the maintenance.

Here's what I see slip after approval:

→ Modifications not filed when pricing changes → now you're out of compliance with your Basis of Award
→ Annual representations not updated → SAM expiration can pull the entire Schedule
→ Trade Agreements Act requirements ignored → one non-compliant product can trigger an audit
→ Mass modifications untracked → GSA sends them; most holders don't realize they've accepted new terms

A GSA Schedule isn't a license you frame on the wall. It's a living contract with real obligations.

The companies generating consistent federal revenue aren't doing it because they got approved. They're doing it because they stayed compliant.
