Before you write off the federal market as "not for companies like mine" — the numbers already answered that objection.

Four myths I hear every single week, killed with data:

**1. "Only the big primes win federal work."**
→ Small businesses pulled $21.9B from DoD alone in Q2 2026. That's USAspending.gov, not a talking point.

**2. "I need past performance before I can win anything."**
→ Teaming agreements exist for exactly this reason. Subcontract first, build your record, then prime. The requirement redirects you — it doesn't disqualify you.

**3. "The GSA Schedule takes too long to pay off."**
→ Approved once, valid 20 years. The companies not chasing recompetes today are the ones who didn't start 18 months ago.

**4. "Federal pricing will hurt my commercial margins."**
→ It's the opposite. GSA locks in a pre-negotiated rate. Good pricing protects your margin. Bad pricing hands the bid to whoever said yes first.

The objection I hear most is usually the one someone used to avoid doing the research.

Numbers don't care about the excuse you built last year.
