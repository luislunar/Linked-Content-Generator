Teaming health check: here's what most subs miss.

The objection I hear every week: "We want to team with a prime, but no one's responding."

Here's why. Most subs show up without the basics a prime needs to say yes fast:

→ A tight capability statement — 2–3 specific strengths, not a list of everything you *could* do
→ NAICS codes that match the opportunity — primes check this before reading anything else
→ Past performance documented — commercial contracts count, but write them up with scopes and dollar values
→ Insurance and bonding capacity in writing — primes can't absorb your risk exposure
→ SAM.gov active and current — a lapsed registration kills the team before the conversation starts

This isn't about credentials. Primes are on deadline. They need a sub who can close fast and not create problems.

Show up with all five and you go from "no response" to "let's talk" in the same week.
