"You need prior federal contracts to qualify for a GSA Schedule."

I hear this in calls every week.

It's not true — and it's costing companies a year or more of wasted runway.

GSA Schedule eligibility has nothing to do with what you've already done for the government. The bar is:

→ 2+ years in business
→ Active SAM.gov registration
→ Documented commercial revenue

That's it.

The Schedule IS the mechanism that opens the federal door — not a credential you earn after you're already through it. Companies stall waiting to be "ready enough." Meanwhile, businesses with identical profiles are building pipeline.

The objection isn't a qualification issue. It's a belief problem.
