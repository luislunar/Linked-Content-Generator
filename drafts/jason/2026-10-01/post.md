Certifications don't win contracts. Your capabilities do — certifications get you in the room.

That sounds obvious. Most companies act like it's not.

I hear the same question every week: "Which certification should I go after first?" Here's what I actually walk through:

→ **HUBZone** — the set-aside rates are real. So is the geographic restriction. Most companies that ask about it don't qualify by address. Check the map before you invest in the application.

→ **SDVOSB / VOSB** — DoD and VA are the power lanes. The VA alone pushed $3.6B to small businesses in Q2 2026. If you serve that space, this one moves the needle.

→ **WOSB / EDWOSB** — active set-asides across 113+ NAICS codes. Underused because most eligible firms don't know the EDWOSB tier exists and what it unlocks.

→ **8(a)** — nine-year program with real mentorship value if you use it. Most companies I talk to applied, got approved, and spent the first 18 months doing nothing with it.

→ **Small Business (no cert)** — not a strategy. A fallback. Contracting officers use it when nothing else fits. Don't build a pipeline on it alone.

The question isn't "which cert?" It's "which agencies are buying in my NAICS codes and what are they requiring for set-asides?"

Start with the agencies. The cert follows.
