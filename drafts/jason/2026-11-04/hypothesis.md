This draft tests whether a numbered objection-kill format — five real prospecting objections each paired with a hard data point — outperforms Jason's recent single-angle myth posts. The govcon-data anchors ($5.7B VA, $23.1B across eight industries in Q3 2026) are specific enough to create a scroll-stop moment and credible enough to share, which should lift reactions from the "wait, that's real?" reflex. The community question is deliberately open-ended ("which one almost stopped you, or drop the one I missed") to invite both experience-shares and objection additions in comments — expected to move the comments score more than a closed-answer question would. Hypothesis: leading with five objections rather than one deep myth-bust increases the odds that any given reader sees their specific blocker reflected, widening the top-of-funnel reaction pool.
