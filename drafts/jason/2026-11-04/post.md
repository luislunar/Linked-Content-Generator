Five objections I hear every week. Here's the number that stops each one cold.

1. "We don't have federal past performance."
That's what teaming agreements are for. Set-asides ring-fence entire contracts from large primes. First performance often comes through a sub role.

2. "We're too small."
The VA awarded $5.7B to small businesses last quarter alone. Define small.

3. "The GSA Schedule process takes forever."
Client effort is ~3 hours. Our team absorbs the 100+. The timeline is the federal review queue — not the prep.

4. "We can't compete with Booz Allen or SAIC."
On the same contract? You don't have to. Set-aside categories legally exclude them from bidding.

5. "Our industry doesn't have a federal market."
IT, consulting, construction, engineering — $23.1B went to small businesses across eight industries in Q3 2026. One quarter.

The objection is real. The math behind it usually isn't.

Which one almost stopped you? Or drop the one I didn't list.
