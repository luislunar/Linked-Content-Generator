This draft tests whether anchoring on a real, attributable agency figure ($5.7B VA small business awards, Q3 2026) can dismantle the "certifications don't matter much" objection more decisively than a process-based explanation. The VA angle is deliberate: veteran-owned business owners are likely to stop scrolling on a stat tied directly to their set-aside category, boosting reactions. The core tension — SDVOSB status is real access but useless without a procurement vehicle — is designed to surface comments from owners who have certification but no federal revenue, the exact ICP pain point that leads to discovery calls. The community question ("what's the gap between your certification and your first federal award?") invites a specific, vulnerable answer rather than generic agreement, which should lift comment rate. Expected score movers: reactions (ICP recognition) and comments (pain-point activation from the certification-without-vehicle gap).
