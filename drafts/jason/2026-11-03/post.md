"SDVOSB certification barely moves the needle."

I hear that at least twice a week on prospecting calls.

Here's the data that ends the conversation: $5.7 billion.

That's what the Department of Veterans Affairs paid to small businesses in Q3 2026 alone. One quarter. One agency.

The VA is legally required to prioritize SDVOSB-certified companies. That's not a guideline — it's in the procurement rules.

But the certification doesn't do this by itself:

→ It doesn't place you on VA bidding systems automatically
→ It doesn't put your pricing in front of contracting officers
→ It doesn't substitute for a contract vehicle that lets you actually bid

The businesses capturing that $5.7B have two things: SDVOSB status plus a procurement vehicle — usually a GSA Schedule — that gets them in front of the right buyers at the right price.

Certification opens the lane. The Schedule puts you in the race.

If your company is sitting on SDVOSB status without a contract vehicle, $5.7B moved last quarter and you weren't at the table.
