The most expensive mistake I see isn't a typo on the forms.

It's the pricing strategy.

Companies come in having done their own research — and they've inflated everything by 20–30% to "leave room to negotiate." Smart move in the commercial world. Quiet disaster in federal procurement.

Here's the pattern I watch play out, week after week:

→ The CO benchmarks your GSA prices against your commercial invoices (Basis of Award)
→ Inflated prices fail the comparison — you don't "negotiate down," you get flagged
→ Now you're resubmitting pricing data and adding 4–8 weeks to your approval timeline

The companies that land federal work price it correctly from the start. Not low — correct. There's a difference.

If your strategy is "go high and come down later," you're not protecting margin. You're building in a delay.
