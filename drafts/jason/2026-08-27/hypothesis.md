This draft tests whether a contrarian reframe of teaming — from "fallback" to "primary market-entry strategy" — generates comments from small business owners who recognize their own reactive pattern. The three-bullet structure names the exact behaviors that separate proactive teamers from reactive ones, giving readers a concrete checklist to measure themselves against. The pull-quote card (`single-03-quote`, cream background) breaks the two-day run of dark-bg templates and puts the central claim front and center. Expected score movers: reactions from ICP owners who feel called out by the "we'll team if we have to" framing, and comments from people sharing their current teaming approach or asking how to find prime partners.
