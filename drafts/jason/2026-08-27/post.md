Most small businesses treat teaming as a fallback.

"We'll team if we have to" — after they've already failed to win on their own.

The ones I see landing federal awards are doing the opposite.

→ They identify prime partners before an opportunity exists.
→ They build the relationship when there's no RFP in sight.
→ When the solicitation drops, they already know who's priming and whether there's a spot.

Teaming isn't a backup plan. It's the fastest entry into the market for a company without past performance.

The question isn't "should we team?" It's "who are we teaming with, and what does the agreement already look like?"

What's your current teaming strategy — reactive, or built before the RFP drops?
