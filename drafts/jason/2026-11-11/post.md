"Get every certification before you pursue federal contracts."

Here's what I actually see on calls every week.

→ **8(a)** — sole-source authority up to $4.5M (services). Fastest path to a first award for eligible companies under $2M revenue.

→ **SDVOSB** — if you're a veteran, don't skip this. VA channels billions through these set-asides each quarter.

→ **WOSB** — 83 NAICS codes carry set-aside authority. Strong in professional services and IT consulting.

→ **HUBZone** — 10% price evaluation preference at award. Powerful if your location qualifies; useless if it doesn't.

→ **GSA Schedule alone** — opens federal procurement across every agency. No certification required to get approved.

The objection I hear most: "I'll apply once I have all the certs."

Wrong order. The Schedule doesn't require certification. But the time you spend waiting has a real cost.
