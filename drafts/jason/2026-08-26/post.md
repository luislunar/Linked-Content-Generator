Which certifications actually change your GSA Schedule outcomes — and which ones sound impressive but don't move a dollar?

Here's what I see across evaluations, proposals, and competitive bids with small businesses trying to break into federal contracting:

**Certifications that actually move the needle:**

→ **SDVOSB** — The VA awarded $3.6B to small businesses in Q2 2026 alone (Source: USAspending.gov). Veteran-owned set-asides lock out most of the competition by design. If you qualify and aren't using this, you're leaving a real advantage on the table.

→ **HUBZone** — 10% price evaluation preference. That's not a badge — it's real math in a competitive bid. Worth the zone compliance headache if you qualify.

→ **8(a)** — Sole-source authority up to $4.5M ($7M for manufacturing). The value isn't prestige, it's reduced competition. Competing against 5 companies beats competing against 500.

**The one most people overlook:**

→ **WOSB / EDWOSB** — Real set-asides exist in specific NAICS codes. But most business owners assume they qualify across the board and never actually check. That assumption costs real opportunities.

**What no certification fixes:**

A passive pipeline. These certs open doors — you still have to knock.
