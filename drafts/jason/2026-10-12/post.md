Before you start chasing federal contracts — are you searching in the right places?

I ran through a call last week with a company that had spent three months pursuing federal work. Good solution. Competitive pricing. Zero responses.

Eighty-plus outreach emails to agency contacts. Cold. No context. No targeting.

The problem wasn't their offer. It was the search strategy.

Here's what changed:

→ Switched from keyword guessing to NAICS-code filtering on SAM.gov Sources Sought notices
→ Built a recompete list — opportunities where existing contracts expire in the next 90–180 days and agencies have to re-solicit
→ Pulled 12 months of award history to identify which agencies had already bought their exact service category

Six weeks after the pivot: two relevant solicitations in their inbox. One request for a capability statement from a contracting officer.

That's not luck. That's better input data.

Federal pipeline doesn't come from volume — it comes from finding agencies that are already buying your category and getting in front of them before the RFQ drops.

Most companies skip that middle step. They go from "we want federal work" to "let's send emails." The targeting layer is what they miss.

The emails didn't stop. But now they land somewhere they can actually matter.
