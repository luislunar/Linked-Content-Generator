Before you chase your next federal RFQ — when did you last check your SAM.gov expiration date?

I ask because I hear this at least twice a week.

A company reaches out, ready to submit a proposal. Strong NAICS codes, active GSA Schedule, solid past performance. Then we pull their SAM status.

Expired 47 days ago.

Here's what that means in practice:
→ Active contract payments can freeze immediately.
→ New awards get blocked by the system automatically.
→ The CO sees the status, moves to the next vendor.
→ The fix? 10–14 business days minimum — while your competitors stay active.

The federal system doesn't send a renewal reminder the way your credit card does. It quietly lapses. You keep prospecting as if you're still eligible.

The fix is free. The timing never is.
