This draft tests whether a concrete client failure story — wrong vehicle, 14 months wasted — drives higher comment engagement than abstract vehicle comparison posts. The `question` hook ("before you choose...") creates forward tension, pulling the reader into the story before delivering the breakdown. The `single-05-beforeafter` card makes the before/after contrast immediately scannable, which should lift reactions by validating a pain many readers already feel silently. The community question is deliberately specific (NAICS match + vehicle scope) to attract comments from people currently mid-selection, not just passive validation. Expected score lift: reactions (relatable lost-time pain point) and comments (readers sharing their own vehicle selection regrets or asking for a vehicle recommendation for their industry).
