Before you choose your contract vehicle based on what your competitor uses — hear this first.

Last month I spoke with a small IT firm that had been on OASIS+ for 14 months. Zero awards. Frustrated. They called us thinking their proposal writing was the problem.

It wasn't.

Their work was custom programming. They should have been on GSA MAS under the IT Professional Services SIN. Instead, they were competing against large primes on a vehicle designed for complex, high-dollar engagements — a room they were never going to win in.

Here's the breakdown I walk through on every first call:

→ **GSA MAS** — broad commercial items and services, fast on-ramp, accessible across 24+ federal agencies. Start here if you're a small business entering the federal market.

→ **SEWP V** — IT products and solutions only. If you're not selling hardware or commodity software, this isn't your door.

→ **OASIS+** — complex professional services, longer award cycles, high competition. Better suited to established primes with serious past performance and deep BD capacity.

→ **CIO-SP4** — IT services specifically for HHS. Narrow audience, but the agency spends heavily. Relevant only if health IT is your lane.

The firm shifted to MAS. First award came in month 8.

Choosing the wrong vehicle doesn't just slow you down — it puts you in the wrong room entirely.

What vehicle are you targeting? Have you verified it actually matches your NAICS codes and scope of services?
