Past performance is the wrong reason to stay out of federal contracting.

The objection I hear most from services firms: "We don't have federal past performance, so we can't compete."

That's backwards. I say this on at least three calls a week.

Past performance is what you build once you're in the market — not the ticket to get there. New entrants win federal work every quarter. The barrier isn't history. It's visibility.

Here's what actually blocks companies:
→ No GSA Schedule — no vehicle for agencies to buy through
→ NAICS codes misaligned to what agencies are actually purchasing
→ Zero presence to contracting officers who've never heard of you

Past performance is a downstream problem. Market access is the upstream problem.

If you're using "no past performance" as the reason to wait, you're solving the wrong problem in the wrong order.
