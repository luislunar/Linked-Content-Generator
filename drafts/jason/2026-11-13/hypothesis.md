This draft tests whether a direct contrarian reframe of a common sales objection — delivered from the perspective of a sales rep who fields it on multiple calls weekly — drives higher comment rates than checklist or tips formats. By naming the exact myth ("no past performance") and flipping the causal chain (downstream vs. upstream problem), the post gives cold-start prospects a clear permission structure to act *now*. Expected score movement: comments up (+2–4) from business owners who recognise the objection and either validate it or share their own blocker, and reactions up from practitioners who've argued the same point and want to amplify it. The `single-03-quote` pull-quote card anchors the reframe as a quotable one-liner, which should also lift saves versus a straight checklist card.
