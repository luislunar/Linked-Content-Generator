"OASIS+ is going to replace GSA Schedule."

I hear this at least twice a week.

Here's what that call actually sounds like from my end:

**Ask 01 — Set-aside status?**
No 8(a), SDVOSB, or HUBZone cert? OASIS+ unrestricted has a steep past-performance bar with no entry point for a company new to federal contracting.

**Ask 02 — What industry?**
OASIS+ is professional services. IT products, construction, staffing, manufacturing — that conversation ends in about 30 seconds.

**Ask 03 — What do you actually want to bid on?**
Most small businesses want federal access, not a prestige vehicle. GSA MAS covers 12+ million products and services. No revenue floor. Open to new entrants on day one.

The vehicle follows your business. Not the other way around.
