"Federal contracting is only for the big guys."

The objection I hear most this month.

$14.2 billion.

That's what small businesses collected from federal agencies in Q2 2026 — across just the top 8 industry categories. One quarter. Source: USAspending.gov.

Broken down:

→ $3.6B — computer-related services
→ $2.0B — engineering services
→ $1.9B — construction
→ $1.5B — computer systems design
→ $1.3B — custom programming
→ $1.1B — facilities support

These aren't Booz Allen and SAIC. These are companies with 10, 20, 50 employees — companies that look a lot like the ones I talk to every week.

The market isn't closed to small businesses. Getting properly positioned to access it is the problem — and that's a solvable problem.
