Before you spend 12 months chasing a federal certification, answer one question:

Which agency are you actually targeting — and does it even use that set-aside?

I talk to certified companies every week. The pattern is consistent: cert approved, inbox quiet for three months.

The certification opens a door. It doesn't knock for you.

What I see change for companies that actually win set-asides:

→ They pair the cert with a GSA Schedule — visible on the vehicles contracting officers use to buy.
→ They identify which contracting offices award to their NAICS under that specific set-aside.
→ They treat certification day as day one of a BD sprint, not a finish line.

8(a), SDVOSB, HUBZone, WOSB — all powerful. All underused by companies who stop at the paperwork.
