The question I hear on almost every first call: "Should we go SEWP or GSA MAS?"

Wrong question.

The right one: which vehicle does your actual buyer use?

Last month I was on a call with an IT services firm. They'd spent six months preparing for SEWP-V eligibility. Solid team, solid revenue, motivated to break into federal.

Then we pulled their top five target agencies' procurement history on USAspending.

Every one was buying IT services through MAS SIN 54151S. Not SEWP. Not CIO-SP4. Standard MAS.

They almost spent a year chasing a vehicle their buyers never open.

Here's the quick breakdown for context: SEWP-V is NASA-administered — strong for agencies that specifically designate it. CIO-SP4 runs through NITAAC, built for NIH and HHS-adjacent procurement. MAS is the broadest access point: 24 IT SINs, available to virtually every civilian agency.

The right vehicle isn't the most prestigious one. It's the one your buyers are already using.

Before you commit to any contract vehicle, map your top 10 target agencies and pull their actual procurement history. Sixty minutes on USAspending saves you a year of wrong prep.
