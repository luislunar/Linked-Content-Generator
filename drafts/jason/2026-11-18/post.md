Before you submit to SEWP, OASIS+, and two other contract vehicles at the same time — read this.

GSA awarded $3.0B to small businesses in Q3 2026 alone.

Most of it didn't flow through the specialized vehicles. It flowed through the one most small businesses dismiss because "everyone's on it."

The objection I hear most: "GSA MAS is too crowded to compete."

Here's what the data actually shows:

→ GSA MAS: broadest buyer access, open year-round, largest award volume for small businesses
→ SEWP V: IT hardware-focused, invitation-based entry — not a walk-in application
→ OASIS+: application window has closed for most categories — no entry until a follow-on opens
→ CIO-SP4: NIH-focused IT work, restricted pool, not a general IT play

The mistake isn't which vehicle you pick.

It's splitting your BD time across three vehicles and working none of them properly.

One well-worked Schedule beats five sitting idle.
