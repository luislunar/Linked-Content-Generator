A GSA Schedule evaluates your **commercial** past performance — not federal. Not DoD. Not CPARS scores.

GSA wants to see:
→ 2 years in business
→ Two years of commercial invoices
→ Proof you deliver what you promise to private-sector clients

That's the bar.

The assumption that you need federal experience to enter the federal market is the kind of loop that keeps small businesses locked out forever.

I hear it on calls every week:
*"We don't have any government contracts yet, so we're not ready."*

You're more ready than you think.

Your commercial track record — the clients you've invoiced, the work you've delivered — is exactly what GSA evaluates. They're not looking for CAGE codes from your past. They're looking for a business that performs.

The Schedule isn't the reward for completing government work.
