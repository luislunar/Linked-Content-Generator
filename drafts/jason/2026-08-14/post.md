Three out of five prospects I talk to have a SAM.gov issue they didn't know about.

Not always an expired registration. Sometimes it's wrong NAICS codes. A lapsed renewal nobody flagged. A banking error sitting there quietly blocking payments on the day an award tries to land.

SAM.gov health check — five things I verify on every intro call:

→ Registration status — active, expired, or pending? (Pending can hold up an award)
→ NAICS codes — do they match what you're actually quoting on?
→ CAGE code — tied to the correct entity name and EIN? Any mismatch flags a CO.
→ Points of contact — right people, working email addresses. Stale contacts lose RFQs.
→ Renewal date — the 365-day cycle moves faster than anyone expects

If you can't answer all five in under 60 seconds, those gaps are live right now.

Federal buyers don't chase down errors. They move to the next vendor.
