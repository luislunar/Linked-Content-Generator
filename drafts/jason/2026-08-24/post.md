"Federal contracts are only for the big defense primes."

I hear some version of this on almost every first call.

Then I share one number: DoD alone awarded $17.6 billion to small businesses in Q2 2026. One quarter. One agency.

Not Leidos. Not Booz Allen. Small businesses.

The objection isn't really about market access. It's about paperwork anxiety. Companies that could compete are sitting out because they assume the door is locked. It isn't. The barrier is the administrative burden of getting onto the right contract vehicle — not the market itself.

Here's what I see in calls every week:

→ Companies with $2M–$10M in commercial revenue, fully qualified for GSA Schedule
→ Agencies actively searching for exactly what they sell
→ The gap: no contract vehicle to make the transaction legal

Getting on Schedule doesn't guarantee a contract. Nothing does. But staying off it guarantees you're invisible to every Contracting Officer pulling from the approved vendor list.

$17.6 billion went somewhere last quarter. The question is whether your business is positioned to be found when the next RFQ drops.

What's the objection you believed that almost kept you out of federal contracting?
