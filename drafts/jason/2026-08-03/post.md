Before you submit your GSA Schedule pricing — read this.

A company came to our team with a pricing package they'd spent three weeks building.

On paper, competitive. In practice, they'd priced themselves out of every renewal before they'd started.

The problem: they used their standard commercial rates as the Basis of Award.

That sounds right. It isn't.

Commercial prices drift. Discounts happen. If your Schedule is anchored to commercial rates that drop over time, contracting officers can demand you honor that lower price retroactively.

Here's what a proper pricing review actually covers:

→ Audit every most-favored-customer discount before submitting
→ Identify which commercial transactions actually form your BOA basis
→ Never anchor your Schedule rate to prices you routinely discount elsewhere
→ Build room for CO negotiation — they will push back, and that's expected

They resubmitted with a clean BOA analysis.

Rates came in 12% higher than their original draft — and got approved.

Higher rate, stronger position. Because the number was defensible.

Pricing your GSA Schedule wrong doesn't just cost you today — it creates a trap you'll spend years negotiating out of.
