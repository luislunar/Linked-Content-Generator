Sitting across from a company owner last year, I heard it again.

"We have no past performance. We can't win anything federal."

I've heard some version of that sentence probably 300 times in 18 years. And I get it — the assumption sounds logical. But it misses something most GovCon outsiders don't know: the FAR actually anticipates this problem.

Teaming agreements exist precisely for this scenario. A small business pairs with an established prime contractor. The prime brings the past performance and the federal relationships. You bring the technical capacity, the specialized expertise, or the small business set-aside eligibility. Together, you're competitive on contracts neither of you could win alone.

The firm I'm thinking of — IT services, zero federal history, strong commercial track record — structured a teaming agreement within two months of our first call. Eight months later, they had their first federal award. By the time their GSA Schedule was approved, past performance was no longer a gap. It was a line item.

The door into GovCon isn't always the front door — sometimes it's through a partner who's already inside.
