This draft tests the `story` hook pattern on a `teaming` topic using a first-person narrative format (personal_story type). The hook drops the reader into a real conversation — a prospect voicing the "no past performance" objection — which mirrors a belief many ICP owners hold privately but rarely say out loud. By validating that belief before correcting it, the post earns credibility before making its point.

The teaming topic was chosen because it hasn't appeared in the last five runs, it directly addresses a high-friction objection (the past performance barrier), and it connects to GSA Schedule acquisition in a non-obvious way — making it genuinely educational rather than promotional. The narrative structure (problem → mechanism → outcome → lesson) is designed to maximize comments from readers who either recognize the myth or have navigated this path themselves. The community question is deliberately open-ended to invite both the skeptics ("I still don't get it") and the converts ("this is exactly how I got in") — which should lift both reactions and comments.
