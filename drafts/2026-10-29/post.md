Nobody talks enough about how a teaming agreement can solve the past performance problem in a single move.

I had a client — small IT services firm, roughly $3M in annual revenue — who spent two years trying to crack federal contracting on their own. They submitted proposals on half a dozen RFPs. Got screened out every time. The CO's verdict was always the same: no relevant federal past performance.

They came to us convinced the only path forward was grinding through small contracts one at a time. That road takes years, and it's brutal.

Here's what we actually did.

We identified a prime contractor already holding a GSA Schedule in my client's NAICS code who needed the exact technical capability my client had. We structured a formal teaming agreement — my client came in as the subcontractor doing the specialized work they were already doing commercially, while the prime carried the contracting vehicle and compliance burden.

Eight months after we finalized that agreement, my client had their first federal award on record. A real one.

That past performance reference opened three more opportunities in the next six months. They're now pursuing their own GSA Schedule — with documented federal history behind them instead of starting from zero.

The path into federal contracting isn't always a straight line. Sometimes the fastest route is partnering with someone already on the inside.

If you're stuck on the past performance wall right now, teaming deserves a serious look before you spend another year trying to go it alone.
