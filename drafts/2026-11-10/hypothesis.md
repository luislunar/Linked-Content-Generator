This draft tests a myth-bust hook on the certifications topic using a short inspiration format (~102 words). The opening myth — "you don't need certifications to win federal contracts" — is a belief held by two opposing audiences: those who have a Schedule but skipped certifications, and those waiting to get certified before pursuing a Schedule. Both groups misunderstand the relationship between the two tools, which creates natural tension.

The core thesis ("Schedule is the foundation, certifications are the accelerant") reframes the question from either/or to sequence — a more nuanced and actionable takeaway than a simple fact drop. The closing question ("Which did you pursue first?") asks for real sequence data from the audience, not just agreement, which should drive specific, story-rich comments. Expected lift: comments (ICP sharing their order-of-operations decision) and reactions (recognition from people who picked one and stalled).
