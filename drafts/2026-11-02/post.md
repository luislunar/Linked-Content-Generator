"You don't need past performance to get a GSA Schedule."

I've had to say this more times than I can count.

GSA evaluates your financial stability, your commercial pricing history, and your technical capability — not your federal award record. The Schedule exists specifically for companies trying to *enter* the federal market, not just the ones already in it.

The past performance myth has stopped more qualified companies than any real barrier ever has.
