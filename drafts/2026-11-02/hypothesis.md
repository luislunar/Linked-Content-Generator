This draft tests the `truth` hook pattern ("Nobody talks about...") anchored to a real federal spending figure ($23.1B to small businesses in Q3 2026 alone, from USAspending.gov) for the `pipeline` topic. The hypothesis is that leading with a concrete, surprising dollar figure creates a scroll-stop moment — a number most ICP owners have never heard, reframed not as intimidation but as evidence the market is real and reachable. The post deliberately withholds the action step until after the problem diagnosis (access, not money), which should reward readers who stay to the end. Expected engagement: reactions from business owners who feel the "wait, THAT much?" tension, and comments from people who know the access-barrier problem personally — either still stuck outside, recently solved it, or curious what vehicle they're missing. The community question ("what's been the biggest thing keeping you from getting on a federal contract vehicle?") is designed to surface specific, diverse responses rather than vague agreement.
