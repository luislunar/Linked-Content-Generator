Your WOSB certification didn't win that contract. Your proposal did.

Certifications like 8(a), HUBZone, WOSB, and SDVOSB open a smaller competitive pool — which is genuinely valuable. Fewer competitors. Agencies with set-aside goals to meet.

But smaller competition doesn't mean automatic wins. You still need competitive pricing, a capability statement that speaks to the agency's actual mission, and a proposal that proves your company can deliver. The certification is the credential. The contract still requires the work.

The certification opens the door. You still have to walk through it prepared.

If you've had your certification for a year or more and haven't won a set-aside award yet — the problem isn't the paperwork. It's the strategy on the other side of the door.
