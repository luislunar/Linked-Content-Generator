Before you finalize your GSA price list — have you looked up what your competitors are approved to charge?

Most small businesses negotiate their Schedule pricing in a vacuum. They start from their commercial price list, respond to the GSA contracting officer's pushback, and land somewhere that feels reasonable. Close enough.

The problem: every GSA-approved price is public. The day your contract is awarded, your rates appear on GSA Advantage. Competitors can benchmark against you immediately. Many of them have been doing this long before you applied.

The Schedule Sales Query tool on GSA Advantage shows approved pricing by NAICS code, by vendor, by contract vehicle. It's free. It's updated continuously. And it completely changes how you think about every future price negotiation.

Your rate sheet isn't a private negotiation between you and the CO. It's a published document the entire market can read.
