This post tests a `tips_list` format with an `alert` hook on the `compliance` topic, anchored to a genuinely time-sensitive trigger: the federal fiscal year closes September 30 — five days from today. The hypothesis is that real-time urgency (a specific, imminent deadline) converts passive readers into active commenters better than evergreen advice, because it creates a "this applies to me right now" moment for any GSA Schedule holder.

The hook ("FY2026 closes September 30. That's five days from now.") leads with a hard date rather than a question or myth, which is a departure from recent posts — testing whether stark factual urgency outperforms the story and truth hooks used in the prior two days. The five numbered items are deliberately parallel in structure (verb + object) to aid scannability and give readers a self-assessment checklist they can actually run through. The closing reframe ("the most predictable sales cycle in the world") is designed to shift blame from the market to process, which tends to generate comments from owners who've felt this frustration.

Expected score movers: **reactions** from GSA holders who recognize they're leaving year-end money on the table, and **comments** from both practitioners sharing what they're doing this week and aspiring holders who want to understand how to prepare for FY2027. The community question ("Which of these are you already doing?") uses the action-prompt style to invite specific, granular responses rather than generic agreement.
