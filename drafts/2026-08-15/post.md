Most GovCon companies don't have a pipeline. They have a wish list — a few familiar contracts they refresh on SAM.gov once a month.

Here's what actual pipeline discipline looks like from where I sit:

Pull Sources Sought notices 90 days before procurement closes. Track industry days by agency, not just keyword. Flag every recompete where the incumbent is starting to slip. And set a SAM.gov Saved Search with at least five specific filters — NAICS code, set-aside type, place of performance, award ceiling, and solicitation stage.

None of this is software magic. It's a spreadsheet, a recurring Tuesday review block, and the habit of showing up before the RFQ drops.

The difference between a $0 federal year and your first award usually isn't eligibility. It's whether you were watching.
